## Rules for these articles

- **Honest and safe.** Real methods only, with realistic expectations ("لا يوجد ربح سريع
  مضمون"). Warn about scams. No get-rich-quick claims, no invented income figures.
- **No** gambling or betting, trading signals, price predictions, investment advice, "pay to
  join" schemes, piracy, account-selling, or fake engagement. No religious rulings
  (skip «حلال ام حرام» queries). No mention of the UAE (owner's rule 1).
- **Platform requirements change often.** Verify every requirement, threshold and
  payout method on the platform's official help pages plus one more reliable source, and
  write the year («2026») in the title only when the article really contains current-year
  requirements.
- Cover the whole cluster of related queries from the plan row in headings and FAQs
  (each FAQ `###` should be a real query people type).

  `ways-to-earn-money-online` to new ones when relevant (it may be edited for that).
