At **Maloomah** (maloomah.com), we respect our visitors' privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **You don't need an account** or to enter any personal data to browse the site.
- If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.
- We may collect anonymous statistics, such as visit counts, most-read pages and device type, to improve our content.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as dark mode. The third-party services listed below may use their own cookies.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display ads through Google AdSense. In that case:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Analytics (Google Analytics)

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It collects aggregated information that does not personally identify you. To learn more, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## External links

Our articles may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

## Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.
