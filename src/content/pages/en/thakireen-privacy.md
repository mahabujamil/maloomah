This page explains how **Al-Thakireen (الذاكرين)**, an Android app for daily azkar, prayer times, the Holy Quran and Hisn al-Muslim, handles your data. The app is from the same publisher as **Maloomah**.

## In short

- The app needs no account and sends none of your personal data to any server of ours.
- All your settings and progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some technical data to serve them.

## Data stored only on your device

- **Your name:** if you enter it, it is saved on your device so the app can greet you by name on the home screen and in reminders. You can change or remove it in Settings.
- **Your settings and progress:** such as completed azkar, favourites, the tasbeeh counter, your Quran reading position and bookmarks, font size and theme.
- **Reminders and adhan alerts:** they are scheduled on your device itself and never go through a server.

## Approximate location (optional)

- Approximate location is requested only when you tap "Locate me", to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on your device.
- It is sent to Android's built-in geocoding service (by Google) only to find your city's name. We do not keep it or send it anywhere else.
- You can search for your city by name instead, without granting this permission.

## Other permissions

- **Notifications:** for azkar reminders and adhan alerts.
- **Alarms & reminders:** so the adhan alert arrives exactly on time.
- **Display over other apps (optional):** to show a short zikr card, only if you turn on "Auto azkar" yourself.
- **Orientation sensors:** used on your device only, for the Qibla compass.

None of these permissions sends data to servers of ours.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

- The app shows ads through Google AdMob. There are no ads on the Quran and azkar reading screens.
- Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical data about it (such as model, operating system and approximate location inferred from your IP address) to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings under "Ad privacy options".
- You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

## Deleting your data

We keep no data about you on any server, so uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new features. The date of the latest update appears at the top of the page.
