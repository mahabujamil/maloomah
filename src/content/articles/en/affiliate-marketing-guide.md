Have you ever recommended a product you tried to a friend, and they bought it? If you'd had a "special link" for that product, the company would have paid you a commission! That's the simple idea behind **affiliate marketing**, one of the most popular ways to [make money online](/en/articles/ways-to-earn-money-online/), because you don't need your own product, warehouse or shipping.

## What is affiliate marketing?

**Affiliate marketing** means promoting another company's products with your own unique link and **earning a percentage** of every purchase made through that link.

## How does it work?

1. **Join** a store or company's affiliate programme.
2. **Get unique links** to their products.
3. **Share the links** in your content: an article, a video or a post.
4. When someone clicks your link **and buys** within a set time window, the sale is credited to you.
5. **You get paid** once your balance reaches the payout threshold.

## Popular affiliate programmes

| Programme | What stands out |
| --- | --- |
| **Amazon Associates** | Millions of products, with programmes for many regional Amazon stores |
| **AliExpress** | Very cheap, varied products and a huge audience |
| **Temu and Shein** | Low-priced products and fashion, with partner programmes |
| **Regional stores** | Many regions have their own big retailers with affiliate programmes |
| **Digital products** | Web hosting, software and courses, often with higher commissions |

**Commission rates** vary widely by programme and product category, so read each programme's terms carefully before joining.

## Where should you share your links?

1. **A blog or website:** articles like "The 10 best headphones under $50", reviews and comparisons. One of the strongest methods, because it brings steady visitors from Google.
2. **YouTube:** review and unboxing videos, with links in the description.
3. **TikTok and Instagram:** short videos showing the product in use, with a link in your bio.
4. **Telegram and WhatsApp groups:** for sharing deals and discounts, without spamming members.
5. **Coupon and deals sites.**

> **Did you know?** With Amazon's programme, any purchase a visitor makes within **24 hours** of clicking your link usually counts for you, even if they buy something other than the product you recommended.

## How to get started

1. **Pick a specific niche** you know and enjoy, such as tech, kitchen gadgets or baby products.
2. **Choose your platform:** a blog, a YouTube channel or a TikTok account.
3. **Join one or more programmes** that suit your audience.
4. **Create genuinely helpful content** that helps people choose, not just ads.
5. **Disclose your affiliate links** clearly and honestly; many programmes and laws require it, and it builds trust.
6. **Analyse your results:** which products sell best? Focus on those.

## Mistakes that make beginners fail

1. **Spamming links** everywhere without useful content.
2. **Promoting bad products** just because the commission is high, which destroys your audience's trust.
3. **Not disclosing affiliate links.**
4. **Expecting quick money:** affiliate marketing needs an audience, and building one takes time.
5. **Relying on a single programme.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I do affiliate marketing without an audience?

You can start from zero, but real income comes once you have an audience that trusts you, or visitors from search engines.

### Do I need a website?

Not necessarily. Many affiliates use YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. A website is a strong long-term asset, though, because it attracts search traffic for years.

### How much can I earn?

It depends on your audience size, the products, commission rates and how much people trust your recommendations. Many start small and grow gradually.

## Key takeaway

Affiliate marketing is a smart way to earn online without your own product or stock, by promoting stores like Amazon and AliExpress through unique links. The secret to success is **helpful content and honesty with your audience**; trust is what turns a click into a sale.
