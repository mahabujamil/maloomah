We carry phones in our pockets without a second thought, but **who invented the telephone**? The well-known answer is **Alexander Graham Bell** in 1876. The full story is more dramatic, though: it includes a race to the patent office and other inventors who, many argue, deserve part of the credit. Here's the whole story, from the landline to the mobile phone and the smartphone.

## Who invented the telephone, and when?

The telephone is credited to **Alexander Graham Bell** (1847–1922), a scientist and inventor born in Edinburgh, Scotland, who later lived in Canada and the United States. He had a lifelong interest in sound and hearing: his mother and his wife were both deaf, and he worked as a teacher of deaf students.

- On **7 March 1876**, Bell received US patent **No. 174,465** for a method of transmitting speech electrically.
- Three days later, on **10 March 1876**, he made the first successful call to his assistant **Thomas Watson** in another room, with the famous words: "Mr. Watson, come here, I want to see you."

## How did the first telephone work?

The core idea was simple and brilliant: **turn sound into an electric current, then back into sound**.

1. When you speak, a thin membrane (diaphragm) vibrates with the sound waves of your voice.
2. Those vibrations become **variations in an electric current** that travel along a wire.
3. At the other end, the current moves a similar diaphragm, recreating the sound.

None of this would have been possible without earlier discoveries in electricity and magnetism, which we covered in [who invented electricity](/en/articles/who-invented-electricity/).

## The telephone dispute: Gray and Meucci

Bell wasn't the only one working on the idea:

- **Elisha Gray**, an American inventor, filed a document describing a telephone design at the patent office on the **same day** as Bell's application, **14 February 1876**. Historians have long debated who arrived first, and the court battles ended in Bell's favour.
- **Antonio Meucci**, an Italian inventor living in the United States, developed a voice-transmitting device years before Bell, but he was poor and could not renew his provisional patent filing. In **2002** the US House of Representatives passed a resolution recognising his work and contribution to the invention of the telephone.

So many historians see the telephone, like many inventions, as the product of **several inventors' work**, with Bell the one who secured the patent and turned it into a business.

> **Did you know?** Many historical accounts say that during Alexander Graham Bell's burial in 1922, **telephone service across North America fell silent for a minute** in his honour.

## Who invented the mobile phone?

About a century after Bell, American engineer **Martin Cooper** of Motorola made the first call from a handheld mobile phone on **3 April 1973**, on a New York street. The first commercial handheld mobile phone, the **DynaTAC 8000X**, went on sale in **1983**. It was heavy and expensive, and its battery allowed only a fairly short call.

## Who invented the smartphone?

Many consider **IBM Simon**, released in **1994**, the first smartphone: it combined a phone with a touchscreen, email and simple apps. Then Apple's **iPhone** arrived in **2007** and reshaped the smartphone, followed by the **Android** phones that are so widespread today.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who invented the landline telephone?

The landline is the original telephone patented by **Alexander Graham Bell** in 1876, with contributions from other inventors such as Elisha Gray and Antonio Meucci.

### What were the first words spoken on a telephone?

The best-known account is Bell's sentence to his assistant: "Mr. Watson, come here, I want to see you," on 10 March 1876.

### Who invented the cell phone?

The cell (mobile) phone is credited to **Martin Cooper** and his team at Motorola, who made the first handheld mobile call in 1973.

### Did Bell really invent the telephone?

Bell held the official **patent** and made the first known successful call, which is why he gets the credit. Historians also recognise the contributions of inventors who came before or alongside him, especially Antonio Meucci and Elisha Gray.

## Key takeaway

**Alexander Graham Bell** invented the telephone and patented it on 7 March 1876, making the first call three days later, though historians still discuss the roles of Gray and Meucci. The mobile phone followed with Martin Cooper in 1973, the smartphone in the 1990s, and the rest is the device in your pocket.
