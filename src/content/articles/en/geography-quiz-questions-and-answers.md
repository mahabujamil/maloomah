What is the largest lake in the world? Most students name a famous lake in North America or Africa, and then they're surprised: the answer is the Caspian Sea, which is called a sea but is really a lake. Little surprises like that are what make **geography quiz questions and answers** fun in class, in a school assembly or around the table at home. Here are 44 questions in six groups, from continents and oceans down to easy ones for primary pupils. Many answers come with a line explaining why, because an answer you understand sticks far longer than one you memorise.

## Getting the most out of a geography quiz

Teachers: split the class into two teams, put a world map or globe in front of them, and ask whoever answers to point to the place as well. That one habit turns a quiz into map-reading practice. For an assembly, five questions from a single group are plenty.

Students revising alone: cover the answer, write yours on scrap paper, then check. A few days later, go back only to the ones you missed.

## Continents and oceans

| # | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | How many continents are there? | 7: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia |
| 2 | Which continent is the largest by area and population? | **Asia** |
| 3 | Which is the smallest continent? | Australia (Oceania) |
| 4 | Which continent has no permanent residents? | Antarctica; scientists live there only for limited stays |
| 5 | How many oceans are there? | 5: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic |
| 6 | Which ocean is the largest and deepest? | The Pacific |
| 7 | Which ocean is the smallest? | The Arctic |
| 8 | Roughly how much of Earth's surface is water? | About 71% |

Seven continents is the model taught in most schools, though some countries count the Americas as one. For the reasons behind the different counts, see [how many continents are there in the world](/en/articles/how-many-continents-in-the-world/).

## Rivers, lakes and seas

9. What is the longest river in the world? Answer: the Nile, at about 6,650 km. Some researchers argue the Amazon is longer, depending on where its source is measured from, so sources disagree; the details are in [the longest river in the world](/en/articles/longest-river-in-the-world/).
10. Which river carries the most water? Answer: the Amazon in South America, by a huge margin.
11. What is the largest lake? Answer: the Caspian Sea. It is a closed body of water with no link to the ocean, which is why geographers class it as a lake despite its name.
12. What is the lowest place on dry land? Answer: the shores of the Dead Sea, more than 430 metres below sea level.
13. Where is the deepest point in the oceans? Answer: the Mariana Trench in the Pacific, nearly 11 km down.
14. Which two seas does the Suez Canal connect? Answer: the Mediterranean and the Red Sea. It opened in 1869.
15. Which strait links the Mediterranean to the Atlantic? Answer: the Strait of Gibraltar, between Morocco and Spain.
16. Which strait links the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden? Answer: the Bab el-Mandeb.

## Mountains, deserts and islands

| # | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | What is the highest mountain in the world? | **Mount Everest** in the Himalayas, about 8,849 m |
| 18 | What is the longest mountain range on land? | The Andes in South America |
| 19 | Which Arab countries do the Atlas Mountains cross? | Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia |
| 20 | What is the largest hot desert? | The Sahara in North Africa |
| 21 | What is the largest desert of all, scientifically speaking? | Antarctica, because a desert is defined by low rainfall, not heat |
| 22 | What is the largest island? | Greenland |
| 23 | What is the highest waterfall? | Angel Falls in Venezuela, about 979 m |

Question 21 catches out almost everyone, since most people say the Sahara. Geographers call any region with very little precipitation a desert, hot or cold, and Antarctica is one of the driest places on Earth.

> **Did you know?** Everest's height is measured from sea level. Measured from its base on the ocean floor, Mauna Kea in Hawaii rises more than 10,000 metres, taller than Everest, but most of it is hidden under water.

## The Arab world

24. How many Arab countries are there? Answer: 22, the members of the Arab League.
25. Which Arab country is the largest by area? Answer: Algeria, which is also the largest country in Africa.
26. Which Arab country has the most people? Answer: Egypt.
27. What is the capital of Morocco? Answer: Rabat. Many people guess Casablanca, which is the biggest city but not the capital.
28. What is the capital of Oman? Answer: Muscat.
29. Which river runs through Baghdad? Answer: the Tigris.
30. Which Arab country has coasts on both the Mediterranean and the Red Sea? Answer: Egypt.
31. In which country is the ancient city of Petra? Answer: Jordan, in Ma'an Governorate.

## Countries and capitals of the world

| # | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 32 | What is the largest country by area? | Russia |
| 33 | What is the smallest country? | Vatican City, inside Rome |
| 34 | Which country has the most people today? | India, which overtook China in 2023 according to UN estimates |
| 35 | What is the capital of Brazil? | Brasília, not Rio de Janeiro |
| 36 | What is the capital of Australia? | Canberra, not Sydney |
| 37 | What is the capital of Japan? | Tokyo |
| 38 | What is the 0° line of longitude, and which city does it pass through? | The Greenwich (prime) meridian, which runs through London |

Questions 35 and 36 are quiz favourites because in both countries the capital isn't the best-known city. Both capitals were new cities built in the 20th century for the purpose, rather than one of the older big cities.

## Easy geography questions for primary pupils

39. Where does the Sun rise? Answer: in the east, and it sets in the west.
40. What are the four cardinal directions? Answer: north, south, east and west.
41. What do we call a model of the Earth? Answer: a globe.
42. Which imaginary line divides the Earth into northern and southern halves? Answer: the equator.
43. What colour is water usually shown on a map? Answer: blue.
44. Which tool helps us find directions? Answer: a compass.

## Questions students ask about geography quizzes

### Why do some books give different answers?

Because some figures change as measuring improves. Everest has been re-measured several times, and the lengths of the Nile and the Amazon depend on which point counts as the source. That's why many numbers here say "about". In a quiz, what matters is knowing the name and the idea.

### How can I remember capital cities?

Tie each capital to a picture or a fact: Muscat sits on the sea, Rabat on the Atlantic, Canberra was built to be a capital. You'll find many more short facts like these on the [geography "Did you know?" page](/en/did-you-know/geography/).
