## Medium animal quiz questions with answers

These suit middle-school students:

1. **What is the only mammal capable of true flight?**
   The bat. Some mammals, such as flying squirrels, only glide; bats actually fly by flapping their wings.
2. **How many hearts does an octopus have?**
   Three: two pump blood to the gills, and one pumps it around the rest of the body.
3. **What does a camel store in its hump?**
   **Fat**, not water. It uses the fat for energy when food is scarce.
4. **Is a whale a fish?**
   No. Whales are **mammals**: they breathe air with lungs, give birth to live young and feed them milk.
5. **Which bird can fly backwards?**
   The hummingbird. It beats its wings so fast that it can hover in place and even fly backwards.
6. **What is the giant panda's main food?**
   Bamboo.
7. **What is the fastest bird in the world?**
   The peregrine falcon, which can exceed 300 km/h when it dives on its prey.
8. **Which animal is famous for changing colour?**
   The chameleon.
9. **About how long is an elephant's pregnancy?**
   About 22 months, one of the longest of any animal.
10. **Which group does a frog belong to?**
    **Amphibians**, because it spends part of its life in water and part on land.
11. **What is a shark's skeleton made of?**
    **Cartilage**, a flexible material like the one in human ears and noses, not bone.
12. **How do fish breathe?**
    With gills, which take in oxygen dissolved in the water.

## True or false animal questions

These are great for correcting popular myths:

1. **Bats are blind.** ❌ False. Bats can see, but many rely on echoes in the dark.
2. **Ostriches bury their heads in the sand when scared.** ❌ False. It's a famous myth: ostriches lower their heads to turn their eggs in the nest, or press flat to the ground to hide.
3. **Dolphins are mammals.** ✅ True. They breathe air and feed their young milk.
4. **Penguins are birds.** ✅ True. They have feathers and beaks and lay eggs, but they swim instead of flying.
5. **A spider is an insect.** ❌ False. Spiders are arachnids with eight legs; insects have six.
6. **A camel stores water in its hump.** ❌ False. The hump stores fat.
7. **Giraffes are mammals.** ✅ True.
8. **The blue whale is the largest animal known to have lived on Earth.** ✅ True. It is bigger than any known dinosaur.

## How to run an animal quiz in class

1. **Split the class into teams** of 4 or 5, and give each team an animal name.
2. **Start with the easy questions**, then move to medium and hard ones, and make hard questions worth two points.
3. **Use true-or-false questions** for a quick round where students hold up a green or red card.
4. **Ask for the reason:** give a bonus point to the team that explains why, not just what.
5. **Finish with a surprising fact** from our [animals "Did you know?" page](/en/did-you-know/animals/), so students leave with something new.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the easiest animal quiz questions for kids?

Questions about names, sounds and sizes, such as: What is the largest animal? What is a baby lion called? How many legs does a spider have? They're short, and the answers are easy to remember.

### Is a bat a bird?

No. A bat is a mammal: it gives birth to live young, feeds them milk and has fur, not feathers. It is the only mammal capable of true flight.

### What is the difference between an insect and a spider?

An insect has six legs and a body in three parts (head, thorax and abdomen), and many have wings. A spider has eight legs, a body in two parts and no wings.

### How many questions suit a school assembly quiz?

Four to six short questions are usually enough, because the quiz segment of an assembly lasts about two minutes.

## Key takeaway

**Animal quiz questions and answers** are a fun way to teach children and students science without boredom. Start easy, move to medium and hard questions, use true-or-false rounds to bust common myths, and always ask students to explain the reason. That turns a quiz into a real lesson about the animal world.
