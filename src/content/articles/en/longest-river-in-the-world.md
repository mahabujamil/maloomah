It's a classic school and quiz question: **what is the longest river in the world?** The usual answer is the **Nile**. But did you know some scientists argue that the **Amazon** is longer? Here's the full story, with a table of the world's longest rivers and the longest river on each continent.

## The longest river in the world: the Nile

Most geographical references list the **Nile** as the longest river in the world, at about **6,650 km**. It flows north from the heart of Africa and empties into the **Mediterranean Sea** through Egypt's delta.

The Nile has two main branches:

- **The White Nile** rises in the **Lake Victoria** region of central Africa.
- **The Blue Nile** rises at **Lake Tana** in the Ethiopian highlands.

The two meet at **Khartoum**, Sudan's capital, to form the Nile proper. The Nile basin covers **11 countries**, and most of the water that reaches Egypt comes from the Ethiopian highlands.

## Nile vs Amazon: which is longer?

The **Amazon** in South America is about **6,400 km** long by traditional estimates. Some studies, however, have measured it as **longer than the Nile**, because the result depends on two choices:

- **Where does the river start?** Big rivers are fed by many tributaries, and picking the most distant source changes the length.
- **Where does it end?** The mouths of great rivers are wide and branching, so the end point is hard to fix precisely.

So the **Nile remains the longest in most references**, but the scientific debate is real.

What nobody disputes is that the **Amazon is the largest river by volume**. It carries about **one-fifth** of all the river water that flows into the world's oceans.

> **Did you know?** The **Danube** is the longest river in the European Union, and it flows through **10 countries**, more than any other river in the world.

## The longest river on each continent

- **Africa:** the Nile (about 6,650 km)
- **South America:** the Amazon (about 6,400 km)
- **Asia:** the Yangtze in China (about 6,300 km), also the world's third-longest river
- **North America:** the Mississippi–Missouri system. The **Missouri** on its own is slightly longer than the Mississippi.
- **Europe:** the **Volga** in Russia (about 3,500 km)
- **Australia:** the **Murray** (about 2,500 km)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the longest river in the world?

The Nile, at about 6,650 km in most references, though some scientists argue the Amazon may be longer.

### What is the longest river in Africa?

The Nile, which is also the longest river in the Arab world.

### What is the longest river in Asia?

The Yangtze in China, at about 6,300 km.

### Which is the longest river in the Arab world after the Nile?

The **Euphrates**, about 2,800 km long. It rises in Turkey and flows through Syria and Iraq.

## Key takeaway

The **Nile** is the world's longest river at about 6,650 km in most references. The **Amazon** comes next, and some scientists think it's longer. It's certainly the **largest by volume**. Asia's longest river is the **Yangtze**, Europe's is the **Volga**, and the EU's is the **Danube**.
