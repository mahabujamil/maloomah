What is the **largest animal in the world**? Not the elephant, and not a dinosaur, but the **blue whale**. This ocean giant is the biggest animal alive today, and many scientists believe it's the largest animal that has ever lived. Here are its astonishing numbers, what it eats, and the record holders on land, in the air and in the sea.

## The largest animal in the world is the blue whale

The blue whale is a mammal. It breathes air and nurses its young. Despite the name, it looks more blue-grey than blue underwater.

| Fact | Approximate figure |
| --- | --- |
| Length | up to about 30 m (98 ft) |
| Weight | can exceed 150 tonnes |
| Heart | about 180 kg in a specimen measured in 2014 |
| Length at birth | about 7 m |
| Food per day | up to roughly 4 tonnes of krill |
| Lifespan | about 80 to 90 years, by estimates |

To picture it: a blue whale is about as long as three buses in a row and weighs as much as more than 25 African elephants.

## What does the blue whale eat?

The irony is that the biggest animal on Earth lives on tiny prey: **krill**, small shrimp-like crustaceans just a few centimetres long. Blue whales have no teeth. Instead they have **baleen** plates that work like a sieve. The whale gulps an enormous mouthful of water and krill, pushes the water out, and keeps the food.

> **Did you know?** Blue whale calls are among the loudest sounds made by any animal and can travel hundreds of kilometres underwater, but most are so low-pitched that human ears can't hear them.

## Is the blue whale endangered?

Yes. Commercial whaling in the 20th century pushed blue whales close to extinction, until hunting them was banned internationally in 1966. Their numbers are slowly recovering, but the IUCN still lists them as **Endangered**. Today's main threats include ship strikes, entanglement in fishing gear and ocean noise.

## Largest animal ever: were dinosaurs bigger?

Some long-necked plant-eating dinosaurs, such as **Argentinosaurus**, may have matched or exceeded the blue whale in length, but estimates suggest they were most likely lighter. In 2023 scientists described an extinct ancient whale, **Perucetus**, which they estimated might rival the blue whale's weight. Those estimates are based on a partial skeleton and remain under debate. So the blue whale is still the largest confirmed animal in Earth's history.

Whales can grow this big because water supports their weight, so they don't need massive legs to carry it the way land animals do.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the biggest animal in the world?
The blue whale, at up to about 30 m long and over 150 tonnes.

### What is the largest land animal?
The African elephant. Adult males weigh around 6 tonnes, sometimes more.

### Is the blue whale a fish?
No. It's a mammal that breathes air with lungs, gives birth to live young and nurses them.

### What is the second largest animal in the world?
The fin whale, which can grow to about 25 m long.

### Which animal lives the longest?
Among vertebrates, the Greenland shark is a leading candidate. Some studies estimate it can live more than 250 years.

## Key takeaway

The **largest animal in the world** is the blue whale: around 30 m long, over 150 tonnes, and fed almost entirely on tiny krill. It remains endangered despite a slow recovery. On land, the African elephant is the heavyweight champion, and the giraffe is the tallest.
