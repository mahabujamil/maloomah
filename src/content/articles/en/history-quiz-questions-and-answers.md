**History quiz questions and answers** are one of the most enjoyable ways to learn history: they turn dates and names into a fun challenge for the classroom, the school assembly or a family quiz night. Here are **40 questions** across five themes: ancient civilisations, Arab and Islamic civilisation, journeys and discoveries, inventions through history, and easy questions for primary school. Each answer comes with a short fact so students understand rather than just memorise.

## How to use these history quiz questions

- **In class:** split students into two teams, ask each team a question in turn, and give a point for every correct answer.
- **In a school assembly:** pick 5 or 6 questions from one theme for the quiz segment.
- **For self-revision:** cover the answer column with a sheet of paper, try to answer, then check.

Note: many ancient dates are approximate, because historians estimate them from ruins and inscriptions. That is why you will see "about" before some of them.

## Easy history quiz questions for kids

1. **In which country are the famous pyramids?** Egypt.
2. **Which animal did Arab traders use for caravans across the desert?** The camel, nicknamed the "ship of the desert".
3. **What is the name of the long wall built in China to protect its northern borders?** The Great Wall of China.
4. **What did the ancient Egyptians write on?** Papyrus, made from a plant that grows along the Nile.
5. **What were the kings of ancient Egypt called?** Pharaohs.
6. **What is the picture-writing of the ancient Egyptians called?** Hieroglyphs.

> **Did you know?** The Rosetta Stone is in the **British Museum** in London. It carries the same text in three scripts: hieroglyphic, demotic and ancient Greek. Because scholars could read Greek, they could compare the three versions and crack the hieroglyphs. For more facts, visit the [history "Did you know?" page](/en/did-you-know/history/).

## Tips for running a great history quiz

1. **Mix the levels:** start with easy questions so everyone joins in, then raise the difficulty gradually.
2. **Set a time limit:** give each team 15 to 30 seconds to answer.
3. **Explain after each answer:** one sentence on why the event matters makes the fact stick.
4. **Use a map:** when a question is about a place, ask the student to point to it on the map.
5. **Reward extra knowledge:** give a bonus point to anyone who adds a correct fact about the question.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best history questions for a school quiz?

Questions with one clear, agreed answer work best, such as the names of explorers, inventors and famous cities. Use very ancient dates with care, because historians' estimates differ.

### How can I remember historical dates more easily?

Link each date to a story or a picture, and draw the events on a big **timeline**. Seeing events in order helps you remember them far better than memorising numbers on their own.

### Are these questions suitable for every age?

The last section suits primary school, and the rest suit middle and high school. Teachers can choose the questions that match their curriculum.

### Why do historians write "about" before some dates?

Many events in the ancient world were not recorded with an exact calendar. Scholars estimate their dates from ruins, inscriptions and analysis of materials, so the dates are approximate.

## Key takeaway

**History quiz questions and answers** make learning and revising history fun. Use the 40 questions above in class, in assembly or at home, pick the ones that match your students' level, and add a short fact after each answer to bring the event to life.
