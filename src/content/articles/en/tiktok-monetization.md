A video goes viral and the obvious question follows: did that earn anything? TikTok does pay for views, but **not in every country**, and only for videos that meet its rules. Knowing how to make money on TikTok starts with those rules, then moves on to the other income routes, most of which have nothing to do with view counts or going live. Some creators have built serious incomes, but plenty of the promises floating around are false.

## Does TikTok pay for views?

It does, through the Creator Rewards Program. The programme pays creators for qualified views on videos longer than one minute, so a 20-second clip that goes viral earns nothing from it.

### Creator Rewards Program requirements

To join, you need all of the following:

- You are 18 or older.
- You have at least 10,000 followers.
- Your videos have reached at least 100,000 views in the last 30 days.
- You post original videos longer than one minute.
- You live in a country where the programme is offered. That is currently a limited list, so check inside the app.

The country rule catches many people out. TikTok also changes its requirements and eligible countries from time to time, so the Creator tools section in the app is the best guide.

## How to make money on TikTok beyond views

Creators with real incomes usually combine several sources, and some work in countries the rewards programme hasn't reached.

### LIVE gifts

During a LIVE stream, followers can send virtual gifts that turn into a balance you can withdraw. Going LIVE usually requires a minimum number of followers, and you must be 18 or older to receive gifts.

### Brand deals and sponsorships

Once you have an engaged audience, brands will pay you to feature their products. This is one of the **biggest income sources** for creators, and it's available almost everywhere, because the agreement is between you and the company rather than run through a TikTok programme.

### Affiliate marketing

You show a product in a video, put the link in your bio, and earn a commission on each sale made through it. See our [affiliate marketing guide](/en/articles/affiliate-marketing-guide/).

### Selling your own products and services

TikTok makes a good shop window for a small business: handmade goods, home cooking, courses or design services. A short, catchy clip showing how a dish comes together or how a piece is made can bring in real customers. Need ideas? See our [home business ideas](/en/articles/home-business-ideas/).

### Moving your audience to other platforms

Many creators use TikTok to build an audience and then bring those viewers over to YouTube, where monetisation is open in more countries. The details are in our [guide to making money on YouTube](/en/articles/youtube-monetization/).

## Making money on TikTok without going LIVE

It's entirely possible. Brand deals, affiliate links and product sales don't depend on streaming, and the Creator Rewards Program pays for recorded videos where it's available. LIVE is just one method among many.

## The truth about "getting paid to watch ads"

Apps and ads keep promising money for watching videos or ads on TikTok. Some official versions of the app have offered tiny token rewards in a few countries, but those are nowhere near a real income. Many of the other apps making this promise are **fake or scams**: they harvest your data, charge you fees, or ask you to recruit friends before you can be paid.

One rule settles it: the real money on TikTok comes from creating content, not from watching it.

## Growing an audience on TikTok

The first 3 seconds decide whether a viewer keeps watching, so open with your strongest moment. Pick a clear niche people know you for; an account that posts cooking one day and cars the next confuses followers and the algorithm alike.

Trends and trending sounds are worth using, as long as you add your own twist instead of copying. Post consistently, since that helps the algorithm understand your content. Add on-screen text too, because many people watch with the sound off.

Engage with comments and reply to some with videos. And make every clip deliver value: information, a laugh, an idea or inspiration.

## Questions beginners ask about TikTok money

### How much does TikTok pay per million views?

There's no fixed figure. Earnings vary by country, content type and watch time, and by how many of those views qualify for the programme at all.

### Do I need professional equipment?

No. A smartphone with good lighting and clear sound is enough to start, and good content matters more than gear.

### Can I make money on TikTok with few followers?

Yes, by selling your own products or services and through affiliate links, though bigger opportunities like brand deals grow with your audience.
