**Google AdSense** is the world's best-known advertising programme for website and blog owners. The idea is simple: you place Google ads on your site and receive part of what advertisers pay. But how do you get approved? Why are so many sites rejected? And how do you earn more? Here's a practical guide based on real experience.

## How does Google AdSense work?

1. **Advertisers** pay Google to show their ads.
2. Google shows those ads on **sites in the AdSense network**, matched to the page content and the visitor.
3. The **site owner** receives a share of the revenue when visitors view or click the ads.

## AdSense approval requirements

- **Your own website** that you control and can edit.
- **Original, useful content** you wrote, not copied from elsewhere.
- **Being at least 18 years old.**
- **Following Google's policies:** no adult content, violence, pirated material or promotion of illegal activities.
- **Essential pages:** About, Contact and a Privacy Policy.
- **A clear, easy-to-navigate design** that works well on phones.

## How to apply for AdSense

1. **Sign up** on the AdSense website with your Google account.
2. **Add your site's URL** and choose your country.
3. **Place the AdSense code** in the head section of your pages.
4. **Add an ads.txt file** to your site's root folder; this small file confirms you're authorised to show ads.
5. **Request a review** and wait for Google's decision, which can take anywhere from days to a few weeks.

> **Did you know?** These are exactly the steps we followed for Maloomah: we wrote original content, added privacy and contact pages, then placed the AdSense code and ads.txt file and requested a review.

## Common reasons for AdSense rejection

1. **Low-value content:** too few or too thin pages that don't help visitors.
2. **Copied content** or automatically reworded text from other sites.
3. **Policy violations**, such as pirated content or illegal download links.
4. **Navigation problems:** broken menus or empty pages.
5. **The site being unreachable** during the review.

The fix is usually to **add more quality content**, solve the issue, and apply again.

## How do AdSense payments work?

- When your earnings reach **$10**, Google mails a **PIN** to your address so you can verify it.
- Once your balance reaches the **$100 payment threshold**, you're paid the following month, usually in the second half of the month.
- **Payment methods** depend on your country; bank transfer is the most common.

## Strictly forbidden!

- **Clicking your own ads.**
- **Asking friends or visitors to click ads.**
- **Using tools or services** to fake traffic or clicks.

These violations can get your **account closed permanently**, along with your earnings.

## How to earn more with AdSense

1. **Grow your traffic** with search engine optimisation (SEO), writing about topics people actually search for.
2. **Choose niches advertisers value**, such as finance, technology, education and health.
3. **Write long, genuinely helpful articles** that fully answer readers' questions.
4. **Improve site speed** and the mobile experience.
5. **Turn on Auto ads** and let Google choose placements, or place ad units thoughtfully.
6. **Publish regularly**; active sites attract more visitors.

## Making money with AdSense without a website

You can earn through AdSense without a website via **YouTube**: [YouTube Partner Program](/en/articles/youtube-monetization/) earnings are paid through an AdSense account.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does AdSense pay per 1,000 visits?

There's no fixed number. Earnings depend on your niche, where your visitors are, the ads shown and how visitors interact with them.

### How many articles do I need to get approved?

Google doesn't set an official number, but having **dozens of original, helpful articles** greatly improves your chances.

### Can I apply with a free website?

It's better to have **your own domain**, like maloomah.com; it looks more professional and tends to make approval easier.

## Key takeaway

Making money with Google AdSense starts with a site that offers original, useful content, essential pages and a clear design, then applying for review. After approval, income grows with more visitors from SEO and regular publishing, while strictly following the policies and never clicking your own ads.
