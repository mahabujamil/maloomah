Can a ten-second clip filmed in your kitchen actually pay? On Snapchat it can, but only once your account meets the **Snapchat monetization requirements** that Snap sets. The app is hugely popular in the Gulf, Europe and North America, and many creators want to turn views into income. Below you'll find how Snapchat pays creators today, the requirements as Snap's official help pages list them at the time of writing, other ways to earn on the app, and what to realistically expect.

## How does Snapchat pay creators?

In February 2025 Snapchat folded its separate creator payments into one **unified Monetization Program**. The logic is straightforward. Snap places ads within or between your content, and you receive a share of the revenue those ads bring in. That happens in two places: Spotlight, the feed of short vertical videos that resembles Reels and TikTok, and Public Stories, the Stories a creator shares with a public audience.

The program replaced an older system that paid separately for top-performing Spotlight videos. In practice that means income now follows the ads your audience sees over time, rather than one clip that happens to take off.

## Snapchat monetization requirements in 2026

According to Snap's official help page for the program at the time of writing, an account needs the following:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Followers | At least 50,000 |
| Watch time | About 15,000 hours of view time in the last 28 days, with at least 3,000 hours from Spotlight |
| Age | 18, or the age of majority in your country |
| Country | Living in an eligible country |
| Account | A public profile and Snap Star status |
| Content | Original content that follows the Community Guidelines and monetization content rules |
| Joining | By invitation from Snap |

Two things stand out. First, you can't simply hit the numbers and apply; Snap sends the invitation. Second, watch time carries as much weight as followers, and the reason is commercial. An ad only earns money when someone actually watches it, so an account with a big following and few viewers is of little use to advertisers.

From 7 May 2026, Snapchat added another condition for Spotlight rewards: you need to keep at least **100 hours of total Spotlight view time** over the last 28 days to receive the maximum rewards. The Insights section of the app shows where you stand.

> **Note:** Snap adjusts the numbers and eligible countries from time to time, and many websites still repeat older criteria. Always rely on the Insights section of your own account and Snap's official help pages.

## What is a Snap Star?

A Snap Star is a verified public profile that Snapchat grants to notable creators, shown with a gold star next to the name. It comes with extras such as deeper analytics, and as the table shows, it is one of the program's requirements. The badge can't be bought, and Snap charges no fee for invitations to the program.

## How to earn from Spotlight

Everything that works on Spotlight comes back to one idea: people have to keep watching. A few habits help:

1. Post original short vertical videos that grab attention in the first second.
2. Post consistently, since regular posting is one of the biggest drivers of growth and watch time.
3. Use trending sounds and topics when they genuinely fit your niche, not just because they're popular.
4. Avoid content copied from other accounts or reposted with another platform's watermark, which usually isn't eligible.
5. Turn Spotlight viewers into followers by inviting them to your Public Stories.

> **Did you know?** On Snapchat, as on YouTube, watch time now matters more than raw view counts. A video people watch to the end earns far more hours than one they skip after a second.

## Other ways to make money on Snapchat

Fifty thousand followers takes time, but income doesn't have to wait for the program. Brand deals are the most visible route: many restaurants and shops pay local Snapchat creators to promote them. [Affiliate marketing](/en/articles/affiliate-marketing-guide/) is another, where you share product links and earn a commission on sales, as long as you tell your audience the links are ads.

If you like design, the free Lens Studio software lets you build AR Lenses, and businesses pay for custom ones. You can also sell the skills you already use for your own account, such as photography, video editing or managing accounts for small businesses.

And if you're already making short videos, post them on [TikTok](/en/articles/tiktok-monetization/) and [Instagram](/en/articles/instagram-monetization/) as well. Spreading across platforms diversifies your income and means a rule change on one app doesn't wipe it out.

## Mistakes that can cost you the program

The most common one is buying followers or views. It breaks Snap's rules and can get you removed from the program. Close behind is paying someone who promises to "activate monetization" or get you a Snap Star badge; as mentioned above, these services are fake. The most damaging mistake, though, is sharing your login code or password with anyone claiming to be from Snapchat, because that hands them your whole account.

## Questions creators ask about Snapchat monetization

### How much does Snapchat pay?

There's no fixed rate. Earnings depend on watch time, country, audience and the ads shown, and they vary a lot between accounts and from month to month.

### Can you make money from Snapchat Stories?

Yes, if you're in the Monetization Program: ads between your Public Stories earn you a share of revenue. Private Stories shared with friends don't earn anything.

### Which countries are eligible for Snapchat monetization?

The program runs only in a list of eligible countries, which Snap updates over time. Your account's eligibility is shown inside the app.

### Can you make money on Snapchat without 50,000 followers?

Not from the official Monetization Program. Brand deals, affiliate marketing and selling your services are all open to you before you reach that number, and steady, original posting is what moves you toward the program in the meantime.
