Snapchat is hugely popular in the Gulf, Europe and North America, and many creators want to know the **Snapchat monetization requirements** and how to turn views into income. This guide explains how Snapchat's current monetization program works, its requirements as listed on Snap's official help pages at the time of writing, other ways to earn on the app, and what to realistically expect.

## How does Snapchat pay creators?

Since **February 2025**, Snapchat has combined its creator payments into a single **unified Monetization Program**. Snapchat places **ads** within or between your content in:

- **Spotlight:** short vertical videos, similar to Reels and TikTok.
- **Public Stories:** the Stories a creator shares with their public audience.

The creator then receives **a share of that ad revenue**. The program replaced the older system that paid separately for top-performing Spotlight videos.

## Snapchat monetization requirements in 2026

According to Snap's official help page for the program at the time of writing, you need:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Followers** | At least 50,000 |
| **Watch time** | About 15,000 hours of view time in the last 28 days, with at least 3,000 hours from Spotlight |
| **Age** | 18, or the age of majority in your country |
| **Country** | Living in an eligible country |
| **Account** | A public profile and Snap Star status |
| **Content** | Original content that follows the Community Guidelines and monetization content rules |
| **Joining** | By invitation from Snap |

From **7 May 2026**, Snapchat added a new condition for Spotlight rewards: keep at least **100 hours of total Spotlight view time** over the last 28 days to receive the maximum rewards. You can track this in the Insights section of the app.

> **Note:** Snap adjusts the numbers and eligible countries from time to time, and many websites still repeat older criteria. Always rely on the Insights section of your own account and Snap's official help pages.

## What is a Snap Star?

A Snap Star is a verified public profile that Snapchat gives to notable creators, marked with a gold star. It unlocks extras such as deeper analytics, and it's one of the program's requirements. You can't buy the badge, and Snap **charges no fee** for invitations to the program.

## How to earn from Spotlight

1. **Post original short vertical videos** that grab attention in the first second.
2. **Post consistently:** regular posting is one of the biggest drivers of growth and watch time.
3. **Use trending sounds and topics** in a way that fits your niche.
4. **Avoid copied content** from other accounts or reposts carrying another platform's watermark, which usually isn't eligible.
5. **Turn Spotlight viewers into followers** by inviting them to your Public Stories.

> **Did you know?** On Snapchat, as on YouTube, watch time now matters more than raw view counts. A video people watch to the end earns far more hours than one they skip after a second.

## Other ways to make money on Snapchat

Even before you qualify for the program, there are genuine income routes:

- **Brand deals:** many restaurants and shops partner with local Snapchat creators to promote them.
- **[Affiliate marketing](/en/articles/affiliate-marketing-guide/):** share product links for a commission, and disclose that they're ads.
- **Designing Lenses:** learn the free Lens Studio software and offer AR lens design to businesses.
- **Selling your services:** photography, video editing or account management.

If you make short videos, post them on [TikTok](/en/articles/tiktok-monetization/) and [Instagram](/en/articles/instagram-monetization/) too, to diversify your income.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Buying followers or views:** it breaks the rules and can get you removed from the program.
- **Paying anyone who promises to "activate monetization" or get you a Snap Star badge:** these services are fake.
- **Sharing your login code or password** with anyone claiming to be from Snapchat.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Snapchat pay?

There's no fixed rate. Earnings depend on watch time, country, audience and the ads shown, and they vary a lot between accounts and from month to month.

### Can you make money from Snapchat Stories?

Yes. If you're in the Monetization Program, ads between your Public Stories earn you a share of revenue. Private Stories to friends don't earn money.

### Which countries are eligible for Snapchat monetization?

The program is available only in a list of eligible countries, which Snap updates over time. Your account's eligibility is shown inside the app.

### Can you make money on Snapchat without 50,000 followers?

Not from the official Monetization Program, but you can earn from brand deals, affiliate marketing and selling your services before you reach that number.

Snapchat now pays creators through a unified program that shares ad revenue from Spotlight and Public Stories, and it requires a large audience, high watch time and original content.
