YouTube isn't just a place to watch videos; it has become **a real source of income** for millions of creators around the world. But how does the money actually start? What are the requirements? And can you earn without showing your face? Here's your complete guide.

## How does YouTube pay creators?

The official route is the **YouTube Partner Program (YPP)**. Once you're accepted, YouTube runs ads on your videos, and creators receive **55% of the ad revenue** on regular long-form videos. Payments are made through a **Google AdSense** account.

## YouTube monetisation requirements

To earn from ads, you need one of these two options:

| Requirement | Option 1 | Option 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscribers | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Views | 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months | 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days |

There's also an earlier tier with lower requirements, starting at **500 subscribers**, which unlocks some fan-funding features such as memberships and tips, but not ad revenue.

Other key requirements:

- Following **YouTube's policies** and Community Guidelines.
- Living in a **country where the programme is available**; check YouTube's official list.
- **Linking an AdSense account** and turning on two-step verification.

> **Note:** YouTube's requirements can change, so always check the official help pages for the latest details.

## Ways to make money on YouTube

1. **Ads:** the best-known source, based on views and audience type.
2. **Channel memberships:** fans pay a monthly fee for special perks.
3. **Super Chat and Super Thanks:** viewer tips during live streams or on videos.
4. **YouTube Premium revenue:** a share of subscription fees from Premium members who watch you.
5. **Sponsorships:** brands pay you directly to feature their products.
6. **Affiliate marketing:** product links in your description that earn a commission on each sale. See our [affiliate marketing guide](/en/articles/affiliate-marketing-guide/).
7. **Selling your own products:** courses, books, services or merchandise.

## How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?

There's no fixed number. Earnings depend on:

- **Where your viewers are:** views from countries where advertisers pay more earn more.
- **Your niche:** topics like finance, tech and education often attract pricier ads.
- **Video length, engagement** and the time of year.

That's why earnings per 1,000 views can vary enormously from one channel to another.

## Making money on YouTube without showing your face

If you'd rather stay off camera, these ideas work well:

1. **Voice-over explainer channels:** informative videos narrated over images and graphics.
2. **Screen-recorded tutorials:** teaching software, apps and games.
3. **Storytelling and documentary channels** narrated by voice.
4. **Animation and infographics.**
5. **Cooking channels** that only show hands and food.
6. **Calm, ambient or study-music channels** with beautiful visuals (using properly licensed audio).

> **Watch out:** YouTube doesn't pay for **repetitive, copied or mass-produced** content without real value, even if you use AI tools. Your videos need to add something clear for the viewer.

## Tips to grow your channel

1. **Pick a clear niche** you enjoy and can stick with.
2. **Upload consistently**, at least one video a week.
3. **Invest in titles and thumbnails**; they're the first thing viewers see.
4. **Hook viewers** in the first 30 seconds.
5. **Use the keywords people search for** in your title and description.
6. **Use Shorts** to attract new subscribers.
7. **Reply to comments** and build a community.
8. **Respect copyright:** don't use other people's clips or music without permission, or you may lose monetisation.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to qualify for monetisation?

It varies a lot. Some channels qualify within months; others take a year or more. Consistency and improving quality matter most.

### Do I need expensive equipment?

No. Many successful channels started with a smartphone, a cheap microphone and free editing software. Good audio matters more than an expensive camera.

### Can I make money on YouTube from any country?

Only where the Partner Program is available, which covers most countries. Check YouTube's official list for yours.

## Key takeaway

Making money on YouTube is real. It starts with joining the Partner Program after 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views. Income can come from ads, memberships, sponsorships and affiliate links. Choose a niche you love, upload consistently and deliver real value, and results will follow over time.
