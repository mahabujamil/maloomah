Every year on **5 December** the world marks **International Volunteer Day**, and this year it carries extra weight: the United Nations has declared **2026 the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development**. So we have prepared a complete, ready-to-read **school radio about volunteering**: an introduction, a Quran verse, an authentic hadith, a morning speech, a "Did you know?" segment, volunteering ideas students can actually carry out, a quiz and a closing, plus a table for sharing out the roles.

## Running order for a school radio about volunteering

This order suits a morning assembly of about 10 to 12 minutes. The teacher can drop or shorten any segment to fit the time.

| Order | Segment | Approx. time | Presented by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | 1 minute | Radio presenter |
| 2 | Holy Quran | 1 minute | A student who recites well |
| 3 | Hadith | 1 minute | A student |
| 4 | Morning speech | 2 minutes | A student |
| 5 | "Did you know?" | 1.5 minutes | Two students taking turns |
| 6 | Volunteering ideas | 1.5 minutes | Three students |
| 7 | Short sayings | 1 minute | A student |
| 8 | Radio quiz | 2 minutes | Radio presenter |
| 9 | Closing | 1 minute | Radio presenter |

## Introduction to the school radio about volunteering

> In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.
> Good morning, and welcome to a morning of giving.
> Our respected principal, dear teachers, fellow students: may your morning be full of good things.
> Today's assembly is about a value that makes a community kinder and stronger: **volunteering**, which means giving your time, effort or skills to help others without expecting payment. As International Volunteer Day on 5 December draws near, we present these segments.
> We begin with a verse from the Holy Quran, recited by ……

## Holy Quran segment

The student recites this part of verse 2 of Surat Al-Ma'idah. The Arabic text below is copied exactly from quran.com, and the English meaning is the Saheeh International translation:

> ﴿وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ ۚ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ﴾
> "And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is severe in penalty." [Surat Al-Ma'idah, part of verse 2]

**Presenter after the recitation:** Now the hadith, presented by ……

## Hadith segment

> Abu Hurayrah reported that the Messenger of God (peace be upon him) said: "God helps His servant as long as the servant helps his brother." [Part of a hadith in Sahih Muslim (2699)]

**The meaning in brief:** whoever stands by others and helps them will find God helping them in turn. It is one of the strongest encouragements to volunteer and to serve people.

## Morning speech about volunteering

> Dear classmates,
> Volunteering is not only for adults, and it does not need much money. Each of us has something to give: time, a smile, or a skill in drawing, computers or explaining lessons.
> When a student explains a hard lesson to a classmate, that is volunteering. When we help clean the school yard or decorate our classroom, we are volunteers. When we collect old books to give to people who need them, we make a real difference.
> Volunteering also gives back to us. It teaches responsibility and teamwork, brings new friends, and gives us the good feeling of being useful to our community.
> So let this year's motto be: **"One hour of my time for my school and my community."**

## Did you know? Facts about volunteering

Two students read these facts in turn:

- Did you know that the UN General Assembly established **International Volunteer Day** on 17 December 1985 and set it for **5 December** every year?
- Did you know that the UN declared **2026** the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development, and that the year was officially launched on 5 December 2025?
- Did you know that **2001** was the first International Year of Volunteers declared by the UN?
- Did you know that the UN has its own volunteer programme, **United Nations Volunteers**, set up in 1970?
- Did you know that volunteering does not need money? It can be time, effort or knowledge, such as teaching older people to use a phone.
- Did you know that planting a single tree in the school garden is a volunteer act whose effect lasts for years?

> **Did you know?** The 2026 International Year of Volunteers comes exactly **25 years** after the first one in 2001. The UN linked it to sustainable development because volunteers help with goals such as quality education and protecting the environment.

## Volunteering ideas students can carry out

Three students read these ideas, and the school can pick one to carry out during volunteering week:

| Idea | Where? | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean-up of the school yard or a local park | School or neighbourhood | Responsibility and cleanliness |
| Planting and watering trees and plants | School garden | Love of nature and patience |
| A small library of second-hand books | Classroom or school | Love of reading and sharing |
| A "study buddy" group to help classmates | Classroom | Cooperation and humility |
| Collecting clean clothes and school supplies for families in need | Organised by the school | Compassion and solidarity |
| Visiting older people or writing cards for them | With parents | Kindness and respect |

**Important:** every volunteering activity for students should be supervised by a teacher or a parent and take place somewhere safe.

If your school chooses a clean-up campaign, you will find useful segments in our [school radio about cleanliness](/en/articles/school-radio-about-cleanliness/).

## Short sayings about volunteering

- Volunteering is giving without waiting for anything back, and its effect lasts.
- One hand cannot clap alone; a community rises on the arms of its volunteers.
- An hour of your time can change someone else's day.
- What matters is not how much you give, but that you start.
- Today's young volunteer is tomorrow's responsible leader.

## Closing of the school radio about volunteering

> As we close today's assembly, let us remember that volunteering is not something we celebrate on one day a year, but a good habit we live every day.
> Let us start in our classroom, our school and our homes, and let each of us choose one small act to offer others.
> Thank you for listening, and peace be upon you.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is International Volunteer Day?

It falls on **5 December** every year. The UN General Assembly established it in resolution 40/212 of 17 December 1985.

### What is volunteering, in simple words for students?

Volunteering means giving your time, effort or skills to help others or serve your community by your own choice, without expecting to be paid.

### How long should a school radio about volunteering be?

About 10 to 12 minutes. If time is short, keep the introduction, the Quran, the morning speech, the "Did you know?" segment and the closing.

### Is this script suitable for primary students?

Yes. The short sayings, the quiz and the table of ideas suit younger children, and the morning speech can be simplified for the early grades. For a script on a related value, see our [school radio about tolerance](/en/articles/school-radio-about-tolerance/).

## Key takeaway

A **school radio about volunteering** teaches students that giving does not need money and that an hour of their time can make a real difference. Present these segments in the week before **5 December**, and end the assembly by launching a small volunteer project at your school, especially as **2026** is an International Year of Volunteers.
