International Volunteer Day falls on **5 December**, and this year it matters more than usual: the United Nations has named **2026 the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development**. Below is a full **school radio about volunteering** that a teacher can hand out to students as it stands. It has an introduction, a Quran verse, an authentic hadith, a morning speech, a "Did you know?" segment, volunteering ideas students can really carry out, a quiz and a closing.

## Running order for a school radio about volunteering

This order fits a morning assembly of 10 to 12 minutes. If you're short on time, cut or trim a segment; the message matters more than reading every line.

| Order | Segment | Approx. time | Presented by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | 1 minute | Radio presenter |
| 2 | Holy Quran | 1 minute | A student who recites well |
| 3 | Hadith | 1 minute | A student |
| 4 | Morning speech | 2 minutes | A student |
| 5 | "Did you know?" | 1.5 minutes | Two students taking turns |
| 6 | Volunteering ideas | 1.5 minutes | Three students |
| 7 | Short sayings | 1 minute | A student |
| 8 | Radio quiz | 2 minutes | Radio presenter |
| 9 | Closing | 1 minute | Radio presenter |

## Introduction to the school radio about volunteering

> In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.
> Good morning, and welcome to a morning of giving.
> Our respected principal, dear teachers, fellow students: may your morning be full of good things.
> Today we're talking about something that makes a community kinder and stronger. Volunteering means giving your time, effort or skills to help others without expecting to be paid. We chose it because International Volunteer Day is coming up on 5 December.
> We begin with a verse from the Holy Quran, recited by ……

## Holy Quran segment

The verse chosen is part of verse 2 of Surat Al-Ma'idah, which makes helping one another in good a command from God. The Arabic text is copied exactly from quran.com, and the English meaning is the Saheeh International translation:

> ﴿وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ ۚ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ﴾
> "And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is severe in penalty." [Surat Al-Ma'idah, part of verse 2]

After the recitation, the presenter says: Now the hadith, presented by ……

## Hadith segment

> Abu Hurayrah reported that the Messenger of God (peace be upon him) said: "God helps His servant as long as the servant helps his brother." [Part of a hadith in Sahih Muslim (2699)]

Students grasp the idea quickly: if you stand by other people and help them, God helps you. The time a volunteer gives away isn't lost. That is one of the strongest reasons a believer has to serve others.

## Morning speech about volunteering

> Dear classmates,
> Some of us think volunteering is for grown-ups, or that it takes a lot of money. It doesn't. Each of us has something to give: time, a smile, or a skill in drawing, computers or explaining lessons.
> When a student explains a hard lesson to a classmate the night before a test, that's volunteering. When we help clean the school yard or decorate our classroom, we're volunteers. When we gather old books from home for people who need them, we make a real difference to someone.
> And the good comes back to us too. Volunteering teaches responsibility and teamwork, brings new friends, and leaves us feeling useful to the people around us.
> So let this year's motto be: "One hour of my time for my school and my community."

## Did you know? Facts about volunteering

Two students read these facts, taking turns:

- Did you know that the UN General Assembly established International Volunteer Day on 17 December 1985 and set it for 5 December every year?
- Did you know that the UN declared 2026 the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development, and that the year was officially launched on 5 December 2025?
- Did you know that 2001 was the first International Year of Volunteers declared by the UN?
- Did you know that the UN has its own volunteer programme, United Nations Volunteers, set up in 1970?
- Did you know that volunteering doesn't need money? It can be time, effort or knowledge, such as teaching older people to use a phone.
- Did you know that planting a single tree in the school garden is a volunteer act whose effect lasts for years?

> **Did you know?** The 2026 International Year of Volunteers comes exactly 25 years after the first one in 2001. The UN linked it to sustainable development because volunteers help with goals such as quality education and protecting the environment.

## Volunteering ideas students can carry out

Talking about volunteering is only half of it. Three students read these ideas, and the school picks one to actually do during volunteering week:

| Idea | Where? | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean-up of the school yard or a local park | School or neighbourhood | Responsibility and cleanliness |
| Planting and watering trees and plants | School garden | Love of nature and patience |
| A small library of second-hand books | Classroom or school | Love of reading and sharing |
| A "study buddy" group to help classmates | Classroom | Cooperation and humility |
| Collecting clean clothes and school supplies for families in need | Organised by the school | Compassion and solidarity |
| Visiting older people or writing cards for them | With parents | Kindness and respect |

One rule never changes: **every volunteering activity for students should be supervised by a teacher or a parent and take place somewhere safe.**

## Short sayings about volunteering

- Volunteering is giving without waiting for anything back, and its effect lasts.
- One hand cannot clap alone; a community rises on the arms of its volunteers.
- An hour of your time can change someone else's day.
- What matters is not how much you give, but that you start.
- Today's young volunteer is tomorrow's responsible leader.

## Radio quiz

The presenter reads the questions and students raise their hands. A simple prize works well, such as letting the winner choose which volunteering idea the class takes on:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| On which day is International Volunteer Day? | 5 December |
| Which year did the UN declare the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development? | 2026 |
| Complete the verse: "And cooperate in righteousness …" | "… and piety" |
| Does volunteering always need money? | No, it can be time, effort or knowledge |
| Name one volunteer act you can do at school | Such as cleaning the yard or helping a classmate study |

## Closing of the school radio about volunteering

> As we close today's assembly, let's remember that volunteering isn't something we celebrate on one day a year. It's a habit we can live every day.
> Let's start in our classroom, our school and our homes. Let each of us pick one small thing to do for others, and let this International Year of Volunteers be the start of a small project in our own school.
> Thank you for listening, and peace be upon you.

## Questions teachers ask about this assembly

### When is International Volunteer Day?

It's on **5 December** every year. The UN General Assembly set it up in resolution 40/212 of 17 December 1985, so the week before that date is the natural time for this assembly.

### How do I explain volunteering to students in simple words?

Tell them that **volunteering means giving your time, effort or skills to help others or serve your community by your own choice, without expecting to be paid.** Then give an example from their own classroom, like helping a friend with maths homework.

### How long should it be?

About 10 to 12 minutes. If time is short, keep the introduction, the Quran, the morning speech, the "Did you know?" segment and the closing.

### Does it work for primary students?

Yes. The short sayings, the quiz and the table of ideas suit younger children, and the morning speech is easy to simplify for the early grades. For an assembly on a closely related value, see our [school radio about tolerance](/en/articles/school-radio-about-tolerance/).
