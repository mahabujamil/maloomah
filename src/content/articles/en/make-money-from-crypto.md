"Buy this coin now and it'll grow tenfold in a week!" If you read something like that, be careful straight away. Yes, some people have made money from **cryptocurrency**, but many more have lost theirs through lack of knowledge, greed or fraud. This guide explains the **real ways** to make money with crypto and how risky each one is, with complete honesty.

> **Important:** this article is for education only and is not investment advice. Crypto is one of the riskiest assets there is, and you could lose everything you put in.

## First, understand before you start

If you're not sure what cryptocurrency and blockchain are, start with our guide: [What Is Cryptocurrency?](/en/articles/what-is-cryptocurrency/). The first rule of investing: **never put money into something you don't understand**.

## Ways to make money with crypto

### 1. Long-term investing

You buy a well-known coin and hold it for years, hoping its value rises. Many people use **dollar-cost averaging**: buying a small fixed amount every month instead of investing a lump sum all at once, to smooth out the swings.

- **Risk level:** high. Prices can fall for years before they recover, and may never recover.

### 2. Day trading and speculation

Buying and selling quickly to profit from price moves. It looks tempting, but it's **one of the hardest approaches**, and many studies suggest most individual traders lose money over time.

The most dangerous form is **leveraged trading** (futures), where a small price move can wipe out your entire position.

- **Risk level:** very high.

### 3. Staking

With some coins, you can "lock up" your holdings to help run the network and earn regular rewards. But there are risks: the coin's price can fall by more than the reward, your funds may be locked for a period, and the platform you use could fail.

- **Risk level:** medium to high.

### 4. "Fixed return" and lending programmes

Platforms that promise a yearly return if you deposit your coins with them. Several large platforms of this kind collapsed in 2022, and their users lost their money. **The higher the promised return, the bigger the risk.**

- **Risk level:** high.

### 5. Mining

Running specialised machines to confirm transactions in exchange for rewards. For Bitcoin, mining now requires expensive hardware and very cheap electricity, so it's **usually no longer profitable for individuals at home**.

### 6. Working in the crypto industry

This is one of the **safest options**: instead of investing your money, you sell your skills to crypto companies and projects, in writing, design, programming, community management and marketing. It's a form of [freelancing online](/en/articles/freelancing-for-beginners/).

- **Risk level:** low, but check that whoever you work with is trustworthy.

## The most common crypto scams

1. **"Double your money":** anyone promising guaranteed or large fixed profits is almost certainly running **a scam**.
2. **Pump-and-dump groups:** Telegram and WhatsApp groups that hype a tiny coin, then the organisers sell and leave members with losses.
3. **Fake platforms:** websites that look real, show profits on screen, then refuse withdrawals.
4. **Fake support agents:** someone contacts you and asks for your "recovery phrase" to "help". That's outright theft.
5. **Romance scams:** someone builds a relationship with you online for weeks, then convinces you to invest on a platform they control.
6. **Anonymous new coins:** projects launched with huge hype whose creators then disappear with the money.

> **Did you know?** Legitimate platforms **will never ask** for your recovery phrase or password. Anyone who does is a scammer, however convincing they sound.

## Golden rules to protect your money

1. **Only invest money you could lose completely** without it affecting your life.
2. **Never borrow** to invest in crypto.
3. **Use well-known, licensed platforms** only, and turn on two-factor authentication.
4. **Never share your recovery phrase** with anyone.
5. **Keep large amounts in a cold wallet.**
6. **Avoid leverage** if you're a beginner.
7. **Don't follow social media hype**; do your own research.
8. **Check the law where you live**; rules vary widely between countries.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you make money from crypto without any capital?

The realistic way is to **work** in the industry as a designer, writer, developer or community manager. Offers of free coins for nothing are mostly low-value or scams.

### How much can I make from crypto?

Nobody can promise you numbers. Profits are possible, but losses are just as possible or more so, and anyone guaranteeing profits is lying.

### What's the best cryptocurrency to invest in?

There's no guaranteed answer. Large, established coins are relatively less risky than small new ones, but all of them are volatile.

## Key takeaway

Making money from crypto is possible, but it's **neither easy nor guaranteed**. Long-term investing in small amounts is less risky than day trading, working in the industry is the safest route, and "guaranteed profits" are the number one sign of fraud. Learn first, start small, and never risk money you need.
