As December approaches, searches for **UAE National Day**, now officially called **Eid Al Etihad** (Union Day), climb fast. When exactly is it, why 2 December, and how did the United Arab Emirates come together? Here is the story, short and clear.

## When is UAE National Day?

UAE National Day falls on **2 December every year**. It marks the founding of the **United Arab Emirates** on **2 December 1971**. The occasion was long known as "National Day"; its official name today is **Eid Al Etihad**.

2 December 2026 marks the **55th anniversary** of the union. According to the official public holiday calendar published for the year, the Eid Al Etihad holiday falls on **Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 December 2026**. Authorities sometimes adjust holiday dates, so check government announcements before booking travel.

## How the union was born

Before 1971 the area was made up of several separate sheikhdoms known as the Trucial States. When Britain announced it would withdraw from the Gulf, the rulers began long talks about forming a single state, led by **Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan**, Ruler of Abu Dhabi, and **Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum**, Ruler of Dubai.

- On **18 July 1971** the rulers agreed to form the union and approved a provisional constitution.
- On **2 December 1971** the new state was declared, made up of **six emirates**: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain and Fujairah.
- On **10 February 1972** **Ras Al Khaimah** joined, completing the seven emirates.

Sheikh Zayed was elected the first President and Sheikh Rashid the Vice President. That is why Sheikh Zayed is remembered as the **Founding Father**.

## The UAE flag and its colours

The **UAE flag** was raised for the first time on 2 December 1971. It has a vertical red band beside three horizontal stripes of green, white and black. These are the **Pan-Arab colours**, and they are commonly explained as growth (green), peace and generosity (white), strength (black) and sacrifice (red).

> **Did you know?** The UAE flag was designed by **Abdullah Mohammed Al Maainah**, who was about 19 years old. He spotted a newspaper call for designs and entered the competition, and his design was chosen from more than a thousand entries.

## Other national days in the UAE

Eid Al Etihad is the biggest national occasion, but not the only one:

- **Union Pledge Day (18 July):** marks the rulers' agreement to form the union and adopt the constitution in 1971. It is a commemoration, not a public holiday.
- **Flag Day (3 November):** introduced in 2012. People raise the flag on government and private buildings at the same time.
- **Commemoration Day:** observed just before Eid Al Etihad, at the end of November or start of December.
- **Eid Al Etihad (2 December):** the high point of the national calendar.

## How the UAE celebrates

- **The official show**, broadcast on TV, tells the story of the union through large-scale performances.
- **Fireworks** light up cities such as Abu Dhabi and Dubai.
- **Homes, cars and streets** are decorated with flags and the national colours.
- **Folk arts** such as the Al Ayyala dance, plus heritage events and festivals.
- **Scarves and outfits** in the flag's colours at schools and workplaces.

## Frequently asked questions

### How old is the UAE in 2026?

The country was founded on 2 December 1971, so on 2 December 2026 it celebrates the **55th** anniversary of the union.

### Is UAE National Day the same as Eid Al Etihad?

Yes. They are two names for the same 2 December occasion. "National Day" was the common name for decades; "Eid Al Etihad" is the official name today.

### Do private sector employees get the National Day holiday?

Traditionally the Eid Al Etihad holiday is announced for both the public and private sectors, but the exact dates and length are confirmed officially each year, so follow the official announcements.

### What is the capital of the UAE?

The capital is **Abu Dhabi**, which is also the largest of the seven emirates.

## Key takeaway

UAE National Day, or Eid Al Etihad, on 2 December celebrates the founding of the United Arab Emirates in 1971 by six emirates, with Ras Al Khaimah joining in 1972. In 2026 the country marks 55 years of union, with a public holiday scheduled for 2 and 3 December and celebrations that retell a story of unity led by Sheikh Zayed and Sheikh Rashid.
