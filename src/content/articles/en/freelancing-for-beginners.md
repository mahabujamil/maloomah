Imagine working from home, choosing your own projects and hours, and working with clients from different countries. That's **freelancing**, and today it's one of the most realistic ways to [make money online](/en/articles/ways-to-earn-money-online/). This guide shows you how to start from zero, step by step.

## What is freelancing?

**Freelancing** means offering your services and skills to different clients **for a fee per project or per hour**, without being a permanent employee of one organisation. Much of it now happens through **online platforms** that connect clients with freelancers and protect both sides.

## The most in-demand freelance skills

| Field | Example services |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Logos, social media posts, brand identities |
| Writing and content | Articles, website copy, ad copywriting |
| Translation | Arabic–English translation, document translation |
| Programming | Websites, apps, online stores |
| Video and audio | Editing, motion graphics, voice-over |
| Digital marketing | Social media management, paid ads, SEO |
| Support and admin | Virtual assistance, data entry, customer service |

> **Tip:** you don't need to master everything. Pick **one skill**, become excellent at it, then expand later.

## The best-known freelancing platforms

- **Fiverr:** a huge global marketplace where you publish "gigs" that clients buy directly.
- **Upwork:** a global project platform where you send proposals for jobs.
- **Freelancer and PeoplePerHour:** other well-known options.
- **Regional platforms:** many regions have their own; in the Arab world, for example, Mostaql and Khamsat are popular.

Every platform takes a **commission** from your earnings, so read the terms before you start.

### How much do freelance platforms take?

| Platform | Freelancer fee (at this article's last update) |
| --- | --- |
| **Fiverr** | A flat 20% of every order |
| **Upwork** | A variable fee of 0% to 15% per contract, shown to you when you submit a proposal |
| **Regional platforms** (e.g. Mostaql, Khamsat) | A percentage of each project or service, listed in each platform's terms |

For example, sell a $100 service on Fiverr and $80 reaches your balance, before any withdrawal fees. Build the fee into your prices from day one, and check each platform's official fee page, because fees can change.

## How to start freelancing

### 1. Choose a skill and learn it well

Start with what you know, or learn an in-demand skill through free courses on YouTube and learning platforms. Give yourself a few weeks of hands-on practice.

### 2. Build a portfolio

Even without clients yet, **create sample work** that shows your level: mock logos, sample articles or a simple website. Show it on platforms like **Behance** for designers and **GitHub** for developers.

### 3. Create a professional profile

- **A clear, professional photo.**
- **A specific headline**, such as "Logo and brand identity designer", not "I do everything".
- **A convincing bio** explaining what you offer and how it helps the client.
- **Samples of your work.**

### 4. Send tailored proposals

Don't send the same reply to every job. Read the brief carefully and write a short proposal that shows you understand the client's need and how you'll deliver.

### 5. Start with reasonable prices

At first, reasonable prices help you win your first projects and reviews. As reviews build up, **raise your rates gradually**.

### 6. Deliver quality on time

Good reviews are your real capital as a freelancer. Deliver on time, communicate respectfully, and do what you promised, plus a little more.

## Common beginner mistakes

1. **Working off-platform** early on, which can break the rules and expose you to scams.
2. **Accepting vague projects** without defining exactly what's needed.
3. **Charging rock-bottom prices forever**, which burns you out and never builds real income.
4. **Giving up after two weeks** without projects. The start is the hardest part; it gets better.
5. **Not improving your skills**; the market keeps changing.

## How do you get paid?

Platforms offer different withdrawal methods, such as **bank transfer**, **PayPal** and **Payoneer**, and availability depends on your country. Check what works where you live before you start, and look into any registration or tax requirements for freelance income.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I start freelancing with no experience?

You can start with little experience if you learn an in-demand skill and build good samples. Real experience comes with your first projects.

### How much can I earn freelancing?

It depends on your skill, its quality, your hours and your reviews. Many people start small, and income grows with experience and reputation.

### Fiverr or Upwork: which is better for beginners?

**Fiverr** suits clearly packaged services at set prices, while **Upwork** suits bigger, proposal-based projects. Many freelancers use both.

### Do you need money to start freelancing?

Usually not much: a decent computer or phone, an internet connection and an in-demand skill are enough. Just remember platform fees and withdrawal costs when you set your prices. Freelancing online is a real path to income that starts with one skill, a portfolio and a professional profile on platforms like Fiverr and Upwork. The beginning may be slow, but consistently delivering quality on time turns your first reviews into a steady income.
