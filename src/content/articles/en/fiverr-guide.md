Plenty of people have a skill that others would happily pay for, such as design, writing, translation or video editing, but no idea where to find those buyers. Learning **how to make money on Fiverr** is one of the simplest answers, because the buyers are already there, from all over the world. Fiverr is a global marketplace where sellers list ready-made services, called gigs, at fixed prices, and buyers order them. What follows covers how to start, what Fiverr charges, how seller levels work and how to get paid, with realistic expectations and no get-rich-quick promises.

## What is Fiverr and how does it work?

Fiverr is a marketplace for freelance services. The name comes from the fact that gigs originally started at **$5**. Today sellers set their own prices, and a single order can run into hundreds or even thousands of dollars.

These are the basic numbers to know before you open an account:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-up | free |
| Minimum gig price | $5 |
| Fiverr's fee | 20% of every order, including tips |
| Clearance period | about 14 days after an order completes, shorter for some top sellers |
| Working language | mostly English |

The fee is the line that catches beginners out. Sell a $10 gig and $8 reaches you; even a buyer's tip loses a fifth. So price your work around what you will actually keep, not the number on the page. If freelancing itself is new to you, read [freelancing for beginners](/en/articles/freelancing-for-beginners/) first.

## Fiverr for beginners: becoming a seller

Setting up takes only a few steps, and it is worth doing them properly:

1. Sign up at fiverr.com and choose "Become a Seller".
2. Complete your profile with a clear, real photo, a short description of your experience, and your languages and skills.
3. Verify your identity when Fiverr asks, using only your real details.
4. Turn on two-factor authentication to protect your account and earnings.

One rule matters more than the rest: **one account per person**. Running several accounts, or signing up with false details, is among the fastest ways to get suspended.

## How to write a gig that sells

Think of your gig as a shop window on a busy street. Buyers walk past dozens of similar ones and stop at the window that tells them, at a glance, what they will get. That is why titles start with "I will…" and promise a specific result, for example "I will translate your English article into Arabic".

Below the title sit three packages, Basic, Standard and Premium. They work best when the differences in scope, delivery time and number of revisions are obvious, so buyers can pick one without a string of questions. The description then answers three things: exactly what the buyer receives, what is not included, and what you need from them to begin. Being that precise saves both sides from misunderstandings later.

Your images and portfolio are the proof, so show real samples of your own work and never copied images. Your tags should be the words buyers actually type into search, since that is how they find you.

On price, start competitively to win your first reviews, then raise your rates as your track record grows. A brand-new profile with no reviews is like a café that opened yesterday: people need a reason to try it first.

> **Did you know?** Your response rate is one of the metrics Fiverr uses to evaluate sellers, so installing the Fiverr app and answering potential clients quickly really pays off.

## Fiverr seller levels and requirements

Fiverr sorts sellers into levels, and higher levels bring more buyer trust and extra perks. According to Fiverr's Help Center, levels are based on several metrics, including success score, rating, response rate, orders, unique clients and earnings.

| Level | Main approximate requirements |
| --- | --- |
| New Seller | where every account starts |
| Level 1 | about 5 orders, 3 unique clients, $400 earned, 4.4+ rating |
| Level 2 | about 20 orders, 10 clients, $2,000, 4.6+ rating |
| Top Rated | about 40 orders, 20 clients, $10,000, 4.7+ rating, plus manual review |

Notice that each level asks for a number of different clients, not just orders. Levels are re-evaluated regularly, and you can drop a level if your metrics slip. The thresholds also change from time to time, so check the levels page in Fiverr's Help Center before relying on them.

## Fiverr withdrawal: getting paid

Money from an order doesn't land in your account the moment you deliver. Earnings are held for a clearance period, usually about 14 days for most sellers, and only then can you withdraw them. Finish an order at the start of the month and you can expect to cash it out around the middle.

Withdrawal options depend on your country. The main ones are **PayPal**, Payoneer and bank transfer, and each has its own fees and minimum amount, so compare them on your earnings page. Fiverr income may also be taxable where you live, so check your local rules.

## The Fiverr affiliate program

What if you have nothing to sell yet? Fiverr Affiliates pays you a commission when someone buys through your link. It suits websites and channels that already cover freelancing, design or marketing. ## Mistakes that get Fiverr accounts banned

Months of work can disappear because of a single one of these:

- Communicating or taking payment outside the platform.
- Asking buyers for their email address or phone number.
- Copying other people's work or selling plagiarised content.
- Buying fake reviews or fake orders.
- Opening more than one account.

Be wary, too, of messages offering to "activate" or "level up" your account for a fee. They are scams.

## Questions new sellers ask about Fiverr

### Can you really make money on Fiverr?
Yes, but it is real work that takes skill and patience. Your first order can take weeks, and no income is guaranteed. Sellers who offer a specific, high-quality service, reply quickly and follow the rules climb the levels as orders and reviews build up.

### Can I sell Arabic services on Fiverr?
Yes. There is demand for Arabic translation, voice-over and copywriting, though you will usually talk to buyers in English.

### What's the difference between Fiverr and Upwork?
On Fiverr, buyers come to your ready-made gig; on Upwork, you usually send proposals to client jobs. Our [Upwork guide](/en/articles/upwork-guide/) covers the other side.
