If you have a skill such as design, writing, translation or video editing, learning **how to make money on Fiverr** is one of the simplest ways to sell it to clients worldwide. Fiverr is a global marketplace where sellers list ready-made services, called gigs, at fixed prices and buyers order them. This beginner's guide covers how to start, what Fiverr charges, how seller levels work and how to get paid, with realistic expectations and no get-rich-quick promises.

## What is Fiverr and how does it work?

Fiverr is a marketplace for freelance services. It got its name because gigs originally started at **$5**. Today sellers set their own prices, which can run into hundreds or thousands of dollars per order.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-up | free |
| Minimum gig price | $5 |
| Fiverr's fee | 20% of every order, including tips |
| Clearance period | about 14 days after an order completes, shorter for some top sellers |
| Working language | mostly English |

New to freelancing in general? Start with [freelancing for beginners](/en/articles/freelancing-for-beginners/).

## Fiverr for beginners: becoming a seller

1. **Sign up** at fiverr.com and choose "Become a Seller".
2. **Complete your profile:** a clear, real photo, a short description of your experience, your languages and skills.
3. **Verify your identity** when Fiverr asks, using only your real details.
4. **Turn on two-factor authentication** to protect your account and earnings.

Crucially, it's **one account per person**. Multiple accounts or false details are among the fastest ways to get suspended.

## How to write a gig that sells

Your gig is your shop window:

- **Title:** starts with "I will…" and promises a specific result, e.g. "I will translate your English article into Arabic".
- **Three packages:** Basic, Standard and Premium, with clear differences in scope, delivery time and revisions.
- **Description:** exactly what the buyer gets, what's not included and what you need from them to start.
- **Images and portfolio:** real samples of your work, never copied images.
- **Tags:** the words buyers actually type into search.

Start with competitive prices to win your first reviews, then raise them as your track record grows.

> **Did you know?** Your response rate is one of the metrics Fiverr uses to evaluate sellers, so installing the Fiverr app and answering potential clients quickly really pays off.

## Fiverr seller levels and requirements

Fiverr ranks sellers into levels. Higher levels earn more buyer trust and extra perks. According to Fiverr's Help Center, levels are based on several metrics, including success score, rating, response rate, orders, unique clients and earnings:

| Level | Main approximate requirements |
| --- | --- |
| New Seller | where every account starts |
| Level 1 | about 5 orders, 3 unique clients, $400 earned, 4.4+ rating |
| Level 2 | about 20 orders, 10 clients, $2,000, 4.6+ rating |
| Top Rated | about 40 orders, 20 clients, $10,000, 4.7+ rating, plus manual review |

Levels are re-evaluated regularly, and you can drop a level if your metrics slip. The thresholds change from time to time, so check Fiverr's levels page in the Help Center before relying on them.

## Fiverr withdrawal: getting paid

After an order completes, earnings are held for a clearance period (usually about 14 days) before you can withdraw them. Withdrawal options depend on your country; the main ones are **PayPal**, **Payoneer** and bank transfer. Each has its own fees and minimums, so compare them on your earnings page.

Remember that Fiverr income may be taxable where you live, so check your local rules.

## The Fiverr affiliate program

No skill to sell yet? **Fiverr Affiliates** pays a commission when someone buys through your link. It suits websites and channels that cover freelancing, design or marketing. Learn the basics in our [affiliate marketing guide](/en/articles/affiliate-marketing-guide/).

## Mistakes that get Fiverr accounts banned

- Communicating or taking payment **outside the platform**.
- Asking buyers for their email address or phone number.
- Copying other people's work or selling plagiarised content.
- Buying fake reviews or fake orders.
- Opening more than one account.

Also beware of messages offering to "activate" or "level up" your account for a fee. They're scams.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you really make money on Fiverr?
Yes, but it's real work that takes skill and patience. Your first order can take weeks, and no income is guaranteed.

### How much does Fiverr take from sellers?
20% of every order, including tips.

### Can I sell Arabic services on Fiverr?
Yes. There's demand for Arabic translation, voice-over and copywriting, though communication with buyers is usually in English.

### What's the difference between Fiverr and Upwork?
On Fiverr, buyers come to your ready-made gig; on Upwork, you usually send proposals to client jobs. See our [Upwork guide](/en/articles/upwork-guide/).

### When can I withdraw my Fiverr earnings?
After the clearance period, which is about 14 days after an order completes for most sellers.

## Key takeaway

You can **make money on Fiverr** as a beginner if you offer a specific, high-quality service, write a clear gig, respond fast and follow the rules. Fiverr keeps 20% of each order, and you climb the seller levels as orders and reviews build up. Start small, be patient, and avoid any shortcut that puts your account at risk.
