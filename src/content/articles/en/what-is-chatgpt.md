**ChatGPT** has become one of the most-used apps on the planet, relied on daily by students, office workers and small-business owners. But what is ChatGPT, exactly? How does it seem to "understand" you? And how do you get the most out of it without falling for its mistakes? Here's a plain-language guide.

## What is ChatGPT?

**ChatGPT** is an AI chatbot made by the US company **OpenAI** and released to the public on **30 November 2022**. You type a question or request in everyday language, and it replies with text that reads much like a person wrote it.

It can explain a topic, summarise a document, translate, draft emails and essays, brainstorm ideas and help with computer code, in English, Arabic and many other languages.

The name has two parts: **Chat**, and **GPT**, short for *Generative Pre-trained Transformer*, the type of AI model it runs on.

## How does ChatGPT work?

ChatGPT is built on a **large language model (LLM)**. In simple terms:

1. **Training:** the model learned from an enormous amount of text, picking up the patterns of language and how words and ideas connect.
2. **Prediction:** when you send a message, it predicts the most fitting next word (strictly, the next piece of a word), then the next, and so on until it has built a full answer.
3. **Human feedback:** its answers were refined with help from human reviewers who rated responses, to make them more helpful and safer.

So it doesn't think the way a person does and has no awareness. It generates text from the patterns it learned. For the bigger picture, read [What is artificial intelligence and how does it work?](/en/articles/what-is-artificial-intelligence/)

> **Did you know?** According to estimates published by the bank UBS in early 2023, ChatGPT reached around 100 million monthly users roughly two months after launch, which UBS called the fastest growth of any consumer app in history.

## How to start using ChatGPT

1. Go to chatgpt.com or download the official app from Google Play or the App Store, checking that the publisher is OpenAI.
2. Sign up with an email address or a Google, Microsoft or Apple account.
3. Type your question in the message box and press send.
4. Keep the conversation going with follow-up questions; it remembers the context within the same chat.

There's a free version anyone can use, plus paid plans with extra features and higher limits. Plan details and prices change from time to time, so check the official site for the current options.

## Tips for writing better prompts

- **Be specific.** Instead of "write about water", try "explain the water cycle to a 10-year-old in 5 bullet points".
- **Give it a role and a tone.** "Act as a patient maths teacher."
- **Ask for a format.** A table, a list, or a short paragraph.
- **Refine through conversation.** "Make it shorter", "give me an example", "explain it more simply".

## ChatGPT's limits and mistakes

Powerful as it is, use it with care:

- **It can be confidently wrong.** It sometimes produces false facts or even references that don't exist, often called "hallucinations". Always check important information against reliable sources.
- **It isn't your doctor or lawyer.** Don't rely on it for important medical, legal or financial decisions.
- **Mind your privacy.** Never share passwords, personal data or confidential work information.
- **Academic honesty.** Use it to understand and revise, not to copy homework, and follow your school's or university's rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ChatGPT free?

Yes. There's a free version open to everyone, along with optional paid plans that offer extra features and higher usage limits.

### Who owns ChatGPT?

It was built by OpenAI, an American AI research company based in San Francisco and founded in 2015.

### Does ChatGPT understand Arabic and other languages?

Yes. It understands and replies in many languages, including Arabic, though its accuracy on specialised topics can be higher in English.

### Is ChatGPT connected to the internet?

The model itself relies on what it learned in training, but some versions can search the web to fetch newer information and show their sources.

## Key takeaway

ChatGPT is an AI chatbot from OpenAI, launched in late 2022 and built on a large language model that generates human-like text. It's an excellent tool for learning, writing and organising ideas, as long as you give it clear prompts, double-check anything important, and keep your sensitive data to yourself.
