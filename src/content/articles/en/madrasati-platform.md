Every school week, hundreds of thousands of students and parents in Saudi Arabia search for the **Madrasati platform**: How do I log in? Where's the homework? Why won't my password work? This guide explains what Madrasati is, what it offers, how each type of user signs in, and how to fix the most common problems.

## What is the Madrasati platform?

Madrasati ("my school" in Arabic) is a **learning management system** launched by Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education in 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, to keep schooling going remotely. It serves public-school students from grade 1 to grade 12, along with teachers, school leaders and parents.

Even after classrooms reopened, the platform stayed central to schooling. It is used for homework, short quizzes, enrichment material and, when needed, live virtual classes.

## What Madrasati offers

The platform brings most of what a student needs outside the classroom into one place:

| Service | What it lets you do |
| --- | --- |
| Virtual classes | Attend live lessons through Microsoft Teams |
| Assignments | Receive, complete and submit homework online |
| Quizzes and tests | Take short quizzes and online assessments |
| Digital lessons | Recorded lessons and enrichment content per subject |
| Timetable | See the class schedule and upcoming tasks |
| Parent follow-up | Check homework, attendance and performance |

The ministry also gives students the **Office 365 Education** suite free of charge, including Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OneNote and Teams, linked to their Madrasati account.

## How to log in to Madrasati

The login address is **schools.madrasati.sa**. The steps differ slightly by user type.

### Student login

1. Open the site and choose **Student**.
2. Sign in with a **Microsoft account**, the Office 365 account the ministry issued to the student.
3. Enter the student's email address, then the password.
4. Once inside, you'll see subjects, assignments and the timetable.

Parents usually get the student's login details through the **Noor system** or from the school.

### Teacher login

Teachers choose **Teacher** and sign in with the credentials the ministry provides. From there they can build lessons, post and grade assignments, and follow their students.

### Parent login

Parents choose **Parent/Guardian** and typically sign in with their **Noor** account, which lets them follow their children's homework, attendance and reports.

> **Did you know?** UNESCO selected Madrasati as one of a handful of leading global models of remote learning during the pandemic, and a 2022 World Bank report found that about 98% of students in the Kingdom had logged in to the platform.

## Common problems and fixes

- **Forgot your password:** use the password-recovery option, or ask the school to reset it, or reset it via Noor.
- **The site won't load:** try an up-to-date browser such as Chrome or Edge, clear the cache, or try again at a quieter time; mornings are busiest.
- **It logs into the wrong Microsoft account:** sign out of any personal Microsoft account in the browser first, or use a private window.
- **An assignment is missing:** check that the teacher has published it and that you've picked the right subject and term.

If the problem persists, the ministry provides technical-support channels, and the school administration can usually help.

## Tips for getting the most out of it

- Set a fixed daily time to check for new assignments.
- Don't leave submissions to the last hour; uploads can slow down at peak times.
- Use recorded lessons for revision, combined with [proven study methods](/en/articles/effective-study-methods/).
- Make sure a parent knows the login details so they can follow along.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the official Madrasati link?
The known login address is schools.madrasati.sa. Be wary of look-alike links in messages, and only enter your details on the official site.

### Is Madrasati free?
Yes. It is free for public-school students in Saudi Arabia, as is the linked Office 365 Education suite.

### Is there a Madrasati app?
You can use the platform in a mobile browser, and join virtual classes through the Microsoft Teams app. Only download apps from official app stores.

### How do I find my child's Madrasati email?
Parents can usually get it from the Noor system or directly from the school.

## Key takeaway

Madrasati is the digital front door for Saudi schoolchildren: homework, quizzes, virtual classes and parent follow-up in one place. Log in only through the official link, keep your account details somewhere safe, and make checking the platform a short daily habit. That alone saves a lot of stress before exams.
