It's seven in the evening, the homework is due by midnight, and the login page keeps rejecting the password. That scene plays out in a lot of Saudi homes every week, which is why hundreds of thousands of students and parents search for the **Madrasati platform**. How do I log in? Where's the homework? Why won't the account work? Here is what Madrasati is, what it offers, how each type of user signs in, and what to do when it misbehaves.

## What is the Madrasati platform?

Madrasati, which means "my school" in Arabic, is a **learning management system** launched by Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education in 2020. It arrived during the COVID-19 pandemic so that lessons could carry on while school buildings were closed. It serves public-school students from grade 1 to grade 12, plus their teachers, school leaders and parents.

You might expect a pandemic tool to fade once classrooms reopened. Madrasati didn't, because the problems it solves never went away: where to post homework, how to run a quick quiz without paper, and how parents can see what is happening at school. Today it is used for assignments, short quizzes, enrichment material and, when needed, live virtual classes.

## What Madrasati offers

The idea is simple. Instead of homework arriving through scattered message groups and printouts, most of what a student needs outside the classroom sits in one place:

| Service | What it lets you do |
| --- | --- |
| Virtual classes | Attend live lessons through Microsoft Teams |
| Assignments | Receive, complete and submit homework online |
| Quizzes and tests | Take short quizzes and online assessments |
| Digital lessons | Recorded lessons and enrichment content per subject |
| Timetable | See the class schedule and upcoming tasks |
| Parent follow-up | Check homework, attendance and performance |

Students also get the Office 365 Education suite free from the ministry: Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OneNote and Teams. It is tied to the same Madrasati account, so one login opens both the homework and the tools to do it.

## How to log in to Madrasati

You sign in at **schools.madrasati.sa**. The steps change a little depending on who is signing in: a student, a teacher or a parent.

### Student login

1. Open the site and choose "Student".
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account, meaning the Office 365 account the ministry issued to the student.
3. Enter the student's email address, then the password.
4. Once inside, you'll see subjects, assignments and the timetable.

Where do those details come from? Parents usually get them through the **Noor system** or from the school office. It's worth writing them down somewhere the student can find too.

### Teacher login

Teachers choose "Teacher" and sign in with the credentials the ministry provides. From there they build lessons, post and grade assignments, and keep track of their classes.

### Parent login

Parents choose "Parent/Guardian" and normally sign in with their Noor account. That gives them their children's homework, attendance and reports without waiting for the next parents' evening.

> **Did you know?** UNESCO selected Madrasati as one of a handful of leading global models of remote learning during the pandemic, and a 2022 World Bank report found that about 98% of students in the Kingdom had logged in to the platform.

## When Madrasati won't cooperate

The most common headache is a **forgotten password**. Use the recovery option on the login page, ask the school to reset it, or reset it through Noor.

If the site won't load at all, switch to an up-to-date browser such as Chrome or Edge and clear the cache. Mornings are the busiest time, so the same attempt may simply work an hour later.

A more confusing problem is the browser signing you straight into the wrong Microsoft account, often a family member's personal one. Sign out of that account first, or open a private window, and try again.

And if an assignment seems to be missing, check that the teacher has actually published it and that you've picked the right subject and term. When none of this helps, the ministry runs technical-support channels, and the school administration can usually sort things out.

## Using it without the last-minute panic

Madrasati works best as a small daily habit. A few minutes after lunch to look for new assignments beats discovering three of them the night they're due. Try not to leave uploads to the final hour either, since things slow down when everyone submits at once.

Recorded lessons are handy for revision before exams, especially alongside [proven study methods](/en/articles/effective-study-methods/). And make sure a parent knows the login details. Having someone follow along with you takes more pressure off than you might think.

## Questions parents and students ask about Madrasati

### What is the official Madrasati link?
The known login address is schools.madrasati.sa. Be wary of look-alike links in messages, and only type your details into the official site.

### Is Madrasati free?
Yes. It is free for public-school students in Saudi Arabia, and so is the linked Office 365 Education suite.

### Is there a Madrasati app?
You can open the platform in a phone browser and join virtual classes through the Microsoft Teams app. Only download apps from the official stores.

### How do I find my child's Madrasati email?
Parents can usually get it from the Noor system or straight from the school.
