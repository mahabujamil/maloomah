In Egypt, a corner shop owner can now say "just send it on InstaPay" and you pay from your phone in a few seconds. **InstaPay is Egypt's official instant money transfer app**, and it moves money between banks, mobile wallets and Meeza cards almost the moment you press send. That convenience is why so many people search for it, usually wanting to know how to sign up, what a payment address is, and how much they can send.

## What is InstaPay, and where did it come from?

InstaPay launched in spring 2022 under the supervision of the Central Bank of Egypt. It runs on the country's **Instant Payment Network**, which is what lets a transfer from your bank account reach an account at a different Egyptian bank, a mobile wallet or a Meeza card straight away. It works at any hour, including weekends and public holidays.

The reason it caught on so fast becomes clear when you remember the old way. A transfer between two different banks often took a day or longer. Now the money lands in moments, so people use the app for everyday things: paying rent, sending pocket money to a son or daughter at university, or settling the bill at a small shop.

## What you can do with the app

Most people use it to send money to a bank account, a mobile wallet or a Meeza card. It can also request money from someone else. They get your request and approve it with a tap, which is handy when a group of friends splits the cost of a gift. You can pay by QR code at shops that accept it, check the balance of your linked accounts, and link accounts from several different banks so you don't need a separate app for each one.

## What is an InstaPay address (IPA)?

"What is my InstaPay address?" is one of the most common questions about the app. An Instant Payment Address, or IPA, is a short name you pick for your account, written as name@instapay. Your account number and card number are long and sensitive, so instead of reading them out to everyone who owes you money, you give them this address.

Each linked account can have its own IPA. People can still pay you with your phone number, account number or wallet number if they prefer.

## How to register for InstaPay

Setting up takes a few minutes:

1. Download the app from Google Play or the App Store, and check that it's the official one.
2. Enter your phone number. It has to be the same number your bank has on file. Then type in the one-time code (OTP) sent to you by SMS.
3. Choose your bank and add your account using the details of its debit card or Meeza card.
4. Create your IPA payment address.
5. Set a PIN. You'll need it to confirm every transaction.

Step 2 is where many people get stuck. If you've changed your number and never told your bank, registration won't work until you update it. Note too that InstaPay is for individuals only; company accounts can't use it.

## InstaPay limits and fees

The Central Bank of Egypt sets the transfer caps and has changed them more than once since launch. These are the most widely reported limits at the time of writing:

| Item | Limit or fee |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum per transaction | EGP 70,000 |
| Daily limit | EGP 120,000 |
| Monthly limit | EGP 400,000 |
| Transfer fee (since April 2025) | 0.1% of the amount, minimum EGP 0.50, maximum EGP 20 |
| Balance inquiries | About 10 free per month, then a small fee |

A quick example shows how the fee works. Send EGP 1,000 and 0.1% of that is EGP 1. Send EGP 50,000 and 0.1% would be EGP 50, but the cap means you pay only EGP 20. Some limits vary from bank to bank and the central bank may change them again, so check the limits screen in the app or your bank's website before a large transfer.

> **Did you know?** InstaPay was completely free for about three years. Its small transfer fee only arrived in April 2025, and even now no single transfer costs more than EGP 20, however large it is.

## Keeping your money safe

Speed cuts both ways. Because the transfer is instant, a mistake is hard to undo, and money sent to the wrong person rarely comes back easily. The single most useful habit is to read the **recipient's name** on the screen before you tap confirm.

The second habit is never to share your PIN or verification code, even with someone who says they're calling from your bank. Banks never ask for them, so a caller who does is almost certainly a scammer. The same goes for messages saying you've won a prize or that your account has been blocked; they exist to get you to type your details into a fake page.

As for the phone itself, only install the app from the official store, keep it updated, and use a screen lock so nobody else can open it if the phone is lost.

## Questions new users ask

### Is InstaPay run by the Central Bank of Egypt?

InstaPay runs on the Instant Payment Network, which is licensed and supervised by the Central Bank of Egypt, and Egypt's banks take part in it.

### Can I send money from InstaPay to Vodafone Cash?

Yes. The app can transfer to mobile wallets, including those run by telecom operators, using the wallet number.

### Why is InstaPay not working?

It can go down briefly for maintenance or when traffic is heavy, or fail because of a weak connection or an outdated version of the app. Update it and try again later. If the problem continues, contact your bank's customer service.

### Can I use InstaPay outside Egypt?

Registration needs an Egyptian mobile number that matches your bank records, because that's where the verification code is sent. Some people living abroad find this hard, so it's best to ask your bank about your situation.
