**InstaPay** is one of the most searched terms in Egypt, and for good reason: it has become the easiest way for millions of people to move money between banks in seconds. So what is InstaPay, how do you sign up, and what are its limits and fees? This guide covers it all in plain language.

## What is InstaPay?

**InstaPay** is Egypt's instant money transfer app. It launched in **spring 2022** under the supervision of the **Central Bank of Egypt** and runs on the country's **Instant Payment Network**. It lets you send and receive money instantly between your bank account and any account at another Egyptian bank, a mobile wallet or a Meeza card, around the clock, including weekends and holidays.

Before InstaPay, transfers between different banks often took a day or longer. Now they land in moments, which is why the app has become part of daily life: paying rent, sending pocket money to family, or settling up with a small shop.

## What can you do with InstaPay?

- **Send money** to any bank account, mobile wallet or Meeza card.
- **Request money** from someone, who approves the request with a tap.
- **Pay by QR code** at shops that accept it.
- **Check the balance** of your linked accounts.
- **Link several accounts** from different banks in one app.

## What is an InstaPay address (IPA)?

A common question is "what is my InstaPay address?" An **Instant Payment Address (IPA)** is a short name you choose for your account, in the form **name@instapay**. Instead of sharing your long account or card number, you share this address and people can pay you with it.

Each account you link can have its own IPA. You can also send money using a phone number, an account number or a wallet number.

## How to register for InstaPay

1. **Download the app** from Google Play or the App Store, and make sure it is the official one.
2. **Enter your phone number.** It must be **the same number registered with your bank**. Then type in the one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS.
3. **Choose your bank** and add your account using the details of its **debit card** or Meeza card.
4. **Create your IPA** payment address.
5. **Set a PIN**, which you will need to confirm every transaction.

The app is for **individuals only**; company accounts can't use it.

## InstaPay limits and fees

The Central Bank of Egypt sets the transfer caps and has changed them more than once since launch. According to the most widely reported limits at the time of writing:

| Item | Limit or fee |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum per transaction | EGP 70,000 |
| Daily limit | EGP 120,000 |
| Monthly limit | EGP 400,000 |
| Transfer fee (since April 2025) | 0.1% of the amount, minimum EGP 0.50, maximum EGP 20 |
| Balance inquiries | About 10 free per month, then a small fee |

Some limits may vary by bank and can change, so check the limits screen in the app or your bank's website before a large transfer.

> **Did you know?** InstaPay was completely free for about three years. Its small transfer fee only arrived in **April 2025**, and even now no single transfer costs more than EGP 20, however large it is.

## Staying safe on InstaPay

- **Never share your PIN or verification code.** Your bank will never ask for them.
- **Check the recipient's name** before confirming. Instant transfers are hard to reverse.
- **Watch out for fake links and messages** claiming you've won a prize or that your account is blocked.
- **Only install the app from the official store**, and keep it updated.
- **Use a screen lock** on your phone so no one else can use the app if it's lost.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is InstaPay run by the Central Bank of Egypt?

InstaPay runs on the Instant Payment Network, which is licensed and supervised by the Central Bank of Egypt, and Egypt's banks take part in it.

### Can I send money from InstaPay to Vodafone Cash?

Yes. The app can transfer to mobile wallets, including those run by telecom operators, using the wallet number.

### Why is InstaPay not working?

The app can go down briefly for maintenance or heavy traffic, or fail because of a weak connection or an outdated version. Update the app and try again later. If the problem continues, contact your bank's customer service.

### Can I use InstaPay outside Egypt?

Registration needs an Egyptian mobile number that matches your bank records to receive the verification code, so some people living abroad find it hard to sign up. It's best to ask your bank about your situation.

## Key takeaway

InstaPay is Egypt's official instant transfer app. Supervised by the central bank, it moves money to any bank or wallet in seconds using a simple payment address instead of an account number. Signing up is easy and fees are tiny, but safety is up to you: guard your PIN and check the recipient's name before every transfer.
