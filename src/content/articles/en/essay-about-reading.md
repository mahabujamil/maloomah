Teachers ask for an **essay about reading** at almost every school level, from upper primary to middle school, because reading is the key that opens the door to every other subject. This article gives you a complete model essay **with an outline, an introduction and a conclusion**, followed by a short version for younger students, some well-known quotes to decorate your writing, and tips for writing the essay in your own words.

## Outline for an essay about reading

Before you write, arrange your ideas into clear points. This outline works for most grades:

| --- | --- |
| Introduction | Reading is food for the mind and a window on the world |
| Point 1 | The place of reading, and the first word revealed in the Quran |
| Point 2 | Benefits of reading for the individual |
| Point 3 | Benefits of reading for society |
| Point 4 | Types of reading and how its tools have changed |
| Point 5 | How to make reading a daily habit |
| Conclusion | An invitation to make books lifelong friends |

## Introduction to the essay about reading

Reading is food for the mind, just as food nourishes the body. A body without food grows weak, and so does a mind without knowledge. Reading is also a window on the world that we can open without leaving our seats: we travel to other countries, live in past centuries and meet the ideas of scientists and writers, all from our own rooms. That is why the old Arabic saying goes: "The best companion at any time is a book."

## Body: the value and benefits of reading

### The place of reading in our lives

Reading holds a special place in Arab and Islamic culture. The very first word revealed in the Quran was a command to read: «اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ», "Recite in the name of your Lord who created" (Surah Al-Alaq, 96:1), followed soon after by «الَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِالْقَلَمِ», "Who taught by the pen" (96:4). This shows that learning begins with reading and writing, and that a nation that reads is a nation that moves forward.

### Benefits of reading for the individual

Reading gives the reader many benefits, including:

- **Sharper thinking:** readers learn to compare ideas and judge them.
- **A richer vocabulary:** readers pick up new words and good style, which shows in how they speak and write, and helps a lot in essay writing.
- **Better memory and concentration:** following a story or a book means paying attention and remembering what happened.
- **Better results at school:** students who read understand their lessons faster and answer questions with confidence.
- **Free time well spent:** instead of hours in front of screens.

### Benefits of reading for society

A reading society is an aware society. Its people know their rights and duties, can hold a real discussion and are not easily fooled by rumours. The nations that advanced in science and industry have always respected books and built libraries. Arab and Islamic civilisation knew this long ago: it had great libraries such as the "House of Wisdom" (Bayt al-Hikma) in Baghdad, a famous centre of translation and learning.

### Types of reading and how its tools have changed

There are different types of reading. In **silent reading** we read with our eyes only, which is faster and helps understanding. In **reading aloud** we use our voice, which trains correct pronunciation and confident speaking. The tools of reading have changed over time, from clay tablets and papyrus to printed books, and now to e-books and audiobooks on phones and computers. But the goal has always been the same: knowledge.

### How to make reading a daily habit

- Set aside **twenty minutes** a day for reading, even if it's just before bed.
- Start with books you enjoy: stories, simple science books or the lives of famous scientists.
- Visit the school library or a public library, and join reading competitions.
- Read with your family and swap books with friends.
- Keep a small notebook of the best lines you read; it will help with your essays.

> **Did you know?** The House of Wisdom, which flourished in Baghdad in the Abbasid era, is one of the most famous centres of learning in history. ## Conclusion of the essay about reading

In conclusion, reading is not a school task to finish and forget. It is a lifelong friend. Whoever reads widens their mind, improves their speech and finds their way to success. Let us make a book our companion every day, and remember that a nation that reads never falls behind.

## A short essay about reading for primary students

> Reading is food for the mind and one of the best hobbies. When I read a story, I feel as if I am travelling to a new place and meeting new friends. The first word revealed in the Holy Quran was "Read", which shows how important reading is.
> Reading helps me learn new words, makes my essays easier to write and strengthens my memory. That's why I read every night before bed and borrow books from the school library.
> I love reading very much, and I advise all my friends to make books their best friends.

## Quotes about reading to decorate your essay

2. Reading is food for the mind, as food is for the body.
3. A book is a window on the world.
4. A nation that reads is a nation that rises.
5. Read a lot and you will know a lot.

## Tips for writing an excellent essay about reading

- Open with a short, attractive introduction, and don't let it run on.
- Give each point its own paragraph, and start each paragraph with a sentence that sums up its idea.
- Quote a verse or a line of poetry, and copy it accurately.
- Vary your linking words: "in addition", "moreover", "on the other hand", "in conclusion".
- Read your essay again at the end to fix spelling and punctuation.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should an essay about reading include?

The main points are an introduction describing reading as food for the mind, then the place of reading, its benefits for the individual and for society, its types and tools, how to make it a daily habit, and a conclusion inviting readers to make books lifelong friends.

### How do I write the introduction to an essay about reading?

Write three or four sentences: compare reading to food or to a window, say that it takes you to new worlds, then end with a well-known line such as "The best companion at any time is a book."

### Who said "The best companion at any time is a book"?

It is half of a line of poetry by the classical Arab poet **Abu al-Tayyib al-Mutanabbi**, and it is often quoted when talking about the value of reading and books.

### How long should a school essay about reading be?

It depends on the grade and the teacher. In primary school, one to three short paragraphs are usually enough. In middle school, an essay of five or more paragraphs following the full outline is better.

## Key takeaway

A good **essay about reading** opens with an attractive introduction, then covers the place of reading, its benefits for the individual and society and how to make it a daily habit, and ends with a conclusion that invites readers to make books lifelong friends. Learn from the model above, but write your essay in your own words: teachers value honest ideas far more than memorised text.
